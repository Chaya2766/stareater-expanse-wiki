Spider Cluster

The Stareater Expanse is a globular cluster consisting of approximately 10 thousand stars adding up to roughly 8400 solar masses, as well as a central black hole of about 800 solar masses.

The densely packed cluster creates ideal conditions for interstellar travel, with stars commonly being as little as 0.1ly apart from their closest neighbours. The ultimate dream globular cluster to settle, at least of those known in the galaxy, would have been Omega Centauri, but the Spider Cluster was simply reached first.
A simple system of x/y/z coordinates is usually used to denote positions within the cluster, where the X axis points towards the galactic center, the Y axis points in the direction in which the galaxy is spinning, and the Z axis points to the galactic north. The center of mass of the cluster is taken to be the 0/0/0 point in this coordinate system, which corresponds closely but not exactly to the central black hole.

The coordinate grid on the model on the right uses light years as a conceptually convenient unit.

Finespun Zero habitat

Finespun Zero is a minimum viable habitat design used at the Finespun mining settlement.

Structure

Finespun Zero uses a torus-shaped hull, whose cross-section is split in half by the floor, providing the inner half of the torus as living space and the outer half as system space for such purposes as power distribution, computation, energy storage, door opening mechanisms, underfloor heating, and also the place to keep and use industrial equipment.

Internal environment

The internal atmosphere is 21kPa of pure oxygen, which delivers the exact same amount of oxygen as standard terran air at standard terran pressure but with none of the other gasses, most notably nitrogen. The oxygen is notably not actually necessary for habitability as majority of the time there are no organics onboard the habitat, but as a byproduct of metal refining it is the most easily available gas produced locally, and it provides the necessary pressure to keep water liquid for low temperature electrolysis of asteroid regolith.

Temperature is vaguely kept some safe margin away from freezing as a necessity for the continued functioning of the station, eg. within a few kelvin of 290K ( 17oC / 62oF ).

Rectenna

outdated, needs to be rewritten to match the microwave phased arrays from the felicity tech tree
A large rectenna is mounted in the center of the station to accept usually about 4 megawatts from the surrounding solar arrays equipped with a compatible microwave phased array. The size and efficiency of the rectenna makes the received 4 megawatts correspond to 509 W/m2 of microwave intensity - hazardous to unshielded organics and electronics but still less intense than local sunlight, and stopped easily by a faraday mesh or metal plating without excessive heating.

The rectenna itself has an efficiency of about 90% so at the baseline 4 megawatts it wastes about 400kW to heating, but its own massive area dissipating the heat into thermal radiation actually cools it enough to deal with up to 11.5 megawatts at just 300 kelvin, up to about 21 megawatts of heating at 350 kelvin. This means that while nominally the rectenna accepts only about 4MW of microwave power, it could easily deal with up to 210MW of microwave power, but the problem at that point becomes actually using that much power within the station and radiating away the resulting waste heat from the equipment using that power. The hull of the Finespun Zero habitat is only big enough to radiate 4MW of excess heat, or maybe up to 15MW if the surface is purposefully roughened, before additional radiators need to be installed to keep the interior from reaching unlivable temperatures.

The illustration image was taken by administrator Felicity a few hundred seconds before losing one of her remote bodies to the microwave beam.

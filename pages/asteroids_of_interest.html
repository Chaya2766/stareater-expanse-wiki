Asteroids of interest

This page collects interesting asteroids, particularly those of interest for mining and/or settling by enthusiasts. Among the primary criteria to be found on this list, is that the asteroid should be massive enough to have its own hill sphere. It is expected that the objects on this list will have some undesirable properties such as high inclination or unsatisfactory composition, by the simple reason that those which did not have any undesirable property had already been taken.

It might be found later that some of the objects included in the list had already been settled, which case they will not be removed, but an effort will be made to make sure that the names of such objects are clearly marked, and if possible link to the relevant page.

Note: those are real asteroids, not objects made up for writing purposes, but their listed properties might be overly certain, and some (especially density) might be guessed or simply made up, because our modern data is incomplete and uncertain, whereas in-universe the sizes, shapes, masses and densities of these objects would likely be known exactly.

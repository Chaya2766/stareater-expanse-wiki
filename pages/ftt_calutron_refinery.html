Felicity Techtree: Calutron refinery

A universal refining process that takes separates any input material into isotopically pure elements at the cost of extreme energy inefficiency, used in the Felicity tech tree. A perfect match for Ion Beam Printing. This is pretty much the pinicle of Felicity's design philosophy of picking the simplest, most universal method as long as it is even just barely usable.

A document with math behind the technology is here.

Calutron

The machine takes up a space about 30m by 15m by 2m.

The interior must be vacuumed out, which is very easy to achieve by just keeping the whole machine outside in a vacuum of space. The most convenient place to put it is some distance away from the collector of a concentrated solar array.

The calutron refinery uses fluoride excimer lasers to ionize the input material, then accelerates the ions and passes them through a magnetic field, which causes the ion stream to deflect, with the exact angle of deflection depending on the mass of the ion - as such the material is sorted exactly by atomic mass, ending up in one of 267 collection tubes.

Up to one megawatt of power can be used. The usual input material is kamacite refined from asteroid regolith to remove most of the unneeded elements, or occasionally the material which is left over after all the kamacite is pulled out of the regolith.

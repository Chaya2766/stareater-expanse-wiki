Inklerian pronouns

A set of pronouns aiming to convey useful information about particular kinds of people.
pronounmeaningconveyed information and usefulness
he / him / his male the subject is a person of male body or male cultural archetype, potentially useful for finding mates or tuning one's social image
she / her / hers female the subject is a person of female body or female cultural archetype, potentially useful for finding mates or tuning one's social image
e / em / eir
they / them / their		 person the subject is a person, this pronoun is used to not needlessly leak additional information about the subject
it / it / its nonperson the subject is not a person, used to convey that it cannot suffer harm or otherwise does not have moral weight
historically sometimes used on subjects that themselves self-classified as people as a means to deprive them of moral status

ve / vim / vir virtual the subject is a person existing as a software program and either does not have a physical body or having a physical body is optional - useful for setting the expectation that accommodating vir presence requires computational resources and/or bandwidth rather than physical spaces
ze / zeem / zir semi-plural entity The subject does not cleanly fall into either plural or singular and instead exists in some semi-plural state where they display both some qualities of a group and some qualities of an individual. Ze may be a multi-part entity whose individual parts may think and interact independently, possibly a tightly bound convergency or a composite mind. Mainly used to pre-negate the expectation that contact with one part of the entity conveys the shared information to all zir parts, or that ze will be in self-consistent agreement with zirself about any given subject.

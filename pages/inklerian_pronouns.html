Inklerian pronouns

A set of pronouns aiming to convey useful information about particular kinds of people.

They are intended to serve primarily the expression your particular needs and considerations to others, largely in the line of thinking that says the purpose of language is to help others know what to expect.

ve / vim / vir / virself virtual the subject is a person existing as a software program and either does not have a physical body or having a physical body is optional - useful for setting the expectation that accommodating vir presence requires computational resources and/or bandwidth rather than physical spaces
zie / ziem / zier / zierself semi-plural entity The subject does not cleanly fall into either plural or singular and instead exists in some semi-plural state where they display both some qualities of a group and some qualities of an individual. Zie may be a multi-part entity whose individual parts may think and interact independently, possibly a tightly bound convergency or a composite mind. Mainly used to pre-negate the expectation that contact with one part of the entity conveys the shared information to all zier parts, or that zie will be in self-consistent agreement with zierself about any given subject.

Example cases

  • Felicity is a human mind upload, she resides in a massive compute cluster and her interactions with others and the outside world mainly take place through remote bodies. She is effectively a piece of software, but does not view herself as being that primarily, and those who know her tend to expect her to be physically present in some form.

  • ITON is a digital posthuman having undergone cognitive upscaling. Ve is a being of information first and foremost, and doesn't see vir computational substrate as anything more than the means for vim to exist. Vir interactions with the outside world take any form necessary, be it through drones, temporarily appropriating control of networked infrastructure or merely providing instructions to others. It's not entirely accurate to say ve never appears in person - in vir context appearing in person just isn't a useful concept anymore.

  • Serendipity is a merger of a human mind upload and 64 artificial intellects. Those who talk to ziem often find that they are speaking to just a few of zier subminds while the rest of the whole is entirely focussed on something else, yet zie still responds with opinions formulated by zierself as one whole, at least as long as zie had enough time for zier opinions to propagate across zier mind. Zie finds zier nature as a composite mind important to convey, as it is sometimes easy for others to mistake zier subminds for wholly separate people.

  • Anton-34 is a worker droid and doesn't feel much need to convey much information about eirself, but eir legal status as a person is still important. The society e lives in broadly views eir rights as important, and provides em the means to advocate for emself or seek better circumstances should e someday find emself dissatisfied with eir position.

  • EMAS is an expert system managing a small modular power plant. It can take various kinds of information into account and provides a conversational interface for its operation, allowing it to be configured by merely talking to it about the local climate, power demand patterns and answering its questions about logistics or refueling schedules. It can engage in conversation on its own initiative if needed, but despite its natural personlike interface it is ultimately just a collection of decision trees that do not think or feel in any meaningful sense.

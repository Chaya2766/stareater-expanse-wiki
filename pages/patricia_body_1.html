patricia_body_1

Patricia Whateverweaver's open source project of a full artificial body replacement. Although patricia_body_1 was meant to be just a temporary name for the git repository that held the entire project, it ended up never getting changed and ended up being the final name that remained attached to the project all the way to its finalization in the 15th gigasecond.

Patricia herself underwent an operation to replace her own human body with this one only 8 terran years after the repository was created, but the time from the start of the design process to the operation might actually have been as large as 16 years due to previous work done outside of that particular repository.

Muscle architecture

patricia_body_1 uses liquid dielectric cell muscles, a type of artificial muscle derived from dielectric elastomer muscles by replacing the solid elastomer with a liquid.

A pocket of liquid, with conducting plates on either side, constructed such that once squeezed to its limit it still maintains the needed distance between the electrodes to avoid electric breakdown, is able to work like a muscle when the plates are charged to a high voltage.

In patricia's case, out of the dozens of filling fluids that she tested, the best one turned out to be simple purified water, but the performance of the muscles could be theoretically greatly improved by finding a fluid with higher dielectric constant and breakdown voltage.

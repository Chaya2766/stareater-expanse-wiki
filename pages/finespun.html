Finespun

Finespun is a small mining settlement in the solar system asteroid belt, inhabited in majority by UEIA workers and a single human brain emulation known as Felicity.

A unique feature of this settlement is its reliance on a custom ecosystem of technology that all together allows any piece of itself to be produced from just sunlight and asteroid regolith all the while remaining simple enough that every single worker on Finespun understands every single piece of equipment.

Despite its absurd tech ecosystem, the settlement is capable of comfortably maintaining a growth rate of ×20 per gigasecond, and can achieve growth rates as high as ×27 per gigasecond if all the resources are poured into further expanding its power and production infrastructure, while absolutely isolated from the broader economy.

The settlement's growth can be supercharged by direct financial support, which at the extreme allows it to just barely surpass a growth rate of 100% annually, about ×3921498556 per gigasecond - in that arrangement the entirety of the funding is spent on shipments of high purity gallium and arsenic for solar cells, while all other materials are still produced locally as usual.

The 20× and 27× growth rates were determined experimentally on 26-03-2026 by playing the finespun economic simulator (which is currently still work in progress) for 1 gigasecond of ingame time. The annual doubling was found around unix time 1 780 237 925 after the trade mechanic was partially implemented, but the cost of such growth couldn't be determined because I used bulk prices for elements instead of prices for high purity elements.
After implementing autoplay in the finespun economic simulator, which is capable of performing the same actions I would but without getting bored and clicking wait multiple times in a row to make things go faster or making occasional mistakes, the upper bound on finespun's growth rate without financial assistance increased to somewhere a bit less than 15% annually and more than 70× gigasecondly. Maybe this provides some valid empirical data on how much growth rate loss is expected as a result from mismanagement by the human in charge?

Name and flag

The settlement was named Finespun after the loose composition of the asteroid that centers it, and the architecture that would be built within it in the form of a vast web of tunnels, which ended up never actually being built.

The flag of finespun is an abstract depiction of a kamacite crystal, showing off the mineral's 3-way symmetry, chosen for the symbol due to kamacite being the primary building material for nearly all of Finespun infrastructure, by far the most produced material locally, and also a key element of the local technology.

Culture

Finespun formalizes its calendar in terms of Unix Time, with 100-kilosecond days, 1-megasecond weeks and 10-megasecond months (also called short cycles, medium cycles and long cycles to avoid undesired implications), but allows anyone to adhere to their own custom cycle length as long as it is unambiguously defined in terms of unix time.

There is a standard template for work assignments which is meant to be used in most cases, intended to minimize confusion and naturally fill in incidental knowledge gaps.

Finespun officially opened right at the beginning of the 15-gigasecond epoch, with financial backing from ITON, which was spent pretty much entirely on shipments of high purity gallium and arsenic for construction of the settlement's concentrated solar arrays. The funding continued until 32 000 solar arrays were completed, producing a total 8 gigawatts of power, with the gallium and arsenic totaling roughly 5.9 billion dollars equivalent. Further growth was fueled by the settlement's own production, in pretty much complete isolation from the embercold region's economy apart from very occasional exchanges.

The end of funding is visibly reflected in the growth rate as from unix time 15 000 000 000 to 15 469 607 000 the settlement maintained a 100% annual growth rate (×3921498556 growth per gigasecond), but after the funding stopped, the remaining growth progressed at only 9.9% annual growth rate (×20 growth per gigasecond). After reaching kardashev-0.5 power production, the settlement stopped growing as it was considered to have reached final size at that point, and began to produce excess material.

Vast majority of the excess material would end up sold off to Noca Bell which would benefit greatly from the multiple tonnes of isotopically pure elements that the settlement produces every megasecond. If sold at market prices, the settlement's production would bring in about 641.6 million dollar equivalent every megasecond, 1.84 billion $e annually or about 58.239 $e every second on average.

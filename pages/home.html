Home page - Stareater Expanse

Stareater Expanse is my attempt to canibalize some of my many abandoned writing and art projects and include some of the still active ones into one coherent whole.

I insist on phyics-wise realism so a lot of my projects will nonetheless not make it into here, though it is mainly the hard sci-fi I wrote that I care about.
My writing is very focused on being exact, expect a lot of numbers, equations and detailed descriptions of how something works or how it was accomplished in terms of the real laws of physics as I know them. That's the path I charted and I plan on sticking with it, even where the writing could have probably been better in other aspects if I used a little vagueness or disguised the gaps in my knowledge a bit better.
Enjoyers of Orion's Arm should find themselves right at home here despite differences in the exacts. Even though I write based on my own findings, strict adherence to scientific realism forces a lot of compatibility. Other significant influences on my writing include Science and Futurism with Isaac Arthur.

colour-coded elements:

green marking denote relaxed constraint on scientific plausibility, this does not mean I've given up on the hard sci-fi themes, you will not see magic or made up laws of physics in my writing here, it just means there are elements that hardcore hard sci-fi fans might find particularly questionable or lacking satisfying explanations. The reason something would end up marked as this would usually be that I found strict verification of what I'm writing was slowing me down too much or was too difficult and allowed myself to delve into more speculative things without too much verification for the sake of delivering on interesting concepts quicker
abandoned content is grayed out, and represents that the given piece of content had once existed but had been mostly written out by now, yet was also judged still valuable enough to preserve in a way that keeps it visible
Meta content, that is information basing on things not present in the setting or just talking about writing intent, are marked with this colour (this lights up for easier reading when hovered over). This is occasionally useful for immersion reasons and might also make it more clear when I'm writing something that should not be taken as establishing in-lore events.

tool recomendation

My writing is very math-heavy, and for those like me feel drawn to the math and want to do some themselves, I recommend Qalculate which had been my calculator of choice ever since I discovered it. It seamlessly handles units at the input and converts them as needed, so if you're a fellow hard sci fi enthusiast or writer still relying on your system default calculator like I used to, seriously please go at least take a look at it.

I'm not saying that being able to convert units and quantities manually is a useless skill, but once you've gotten proficient at it, it just becomes a constant and tedious waste of time, and you will even miss fewer mistakes by just seeing that the units at the output are wrong. Trust me, if you are like me, you will love it.

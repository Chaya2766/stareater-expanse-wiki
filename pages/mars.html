Mars

Not long after the rise of artificial superintelligence, Mars had been turned into a planetwide archaeological site, and the surface was depopulated.

In spite of the historical terraforming aspirations, Mars remains barren to this day, and access to the surface had been restricted to the general public. A large amounts of orbital infrastructure contributing primarily compute and communication supports the ongoing search and analysis of the traces of life still present in martian rock, with the goal of reconstruction and de-extinction of as many examples of martian biota as possible.

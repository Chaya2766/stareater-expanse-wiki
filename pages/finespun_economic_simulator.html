Finespun economic simulator

A "game" where you take the role of Felicity and direct the industrial processes at the Finespun mining settlement, collecting material and energy to build additional infrastructure and provide computational power for your own mind. In reality it is more so a simulation tool than a game, but many simulation tools make for good games so I might as well call it that.

Note: the HTML of the tables are read off at the start of each time skip to set the internal variables and then updated at the end of each time skip. If you directly edit them through the inspection menu, you will be directly changing the game state. Or at least that is the intent, the game is not functional at all yet unless I forgot to remove this sentence.

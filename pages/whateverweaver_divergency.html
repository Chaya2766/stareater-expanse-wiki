Whateverweaver divergency

The Whateverweaver divergency is a family of iteratively more advanced mind uploads sourced from one human individual. At the moment the family includes the following:
  1. Patricia, the founder of the family. She had been a transhumanist for a long time before the decision to digitize her mind. After the split, she kept working on some of her projects mainly involving bodily modification.
  2. Felicity, a digital transhuman hyperfocussed on technological independence and full understanding of her own critical infrastructure. She is fundamentally just a digital emulation of Patricia's brain with minor additions, and also exists in a convergency with 16 copies of herself.
  3. Serendipity, a posthuman digital superintellect made of 65 individual minds meshed together into one coherent whole, vastly more mentally capable than either Patricia or Felicity but not so advanced as to become completely unrelatable.

Culture

This divergency remains a close group with its own internal culture.

colour-emotion associations

In general, the association of colours with specific emotions or concepts is as weak here as in any other culture that use colours for diverse purposes, but under certain contexts they can have special meanings. They are especially used to convey emotion through glowing and colour-adjustable elements on the bodies of the divergency members.

Custom vocabulary:
  • Elor - a sensation completely distinct from pain but similar to it in effect, which is usually caused by the strength of normally non-noxious stimuli exceeding the brain's rated operating parameters. In a similar manner that "it hurts" describes something that causes pain, one may say "it ervs" to something which causes elor, and "eloric" as equivalent to "painful". Notably elor is different from pain in that pain can be located on the body, whereas elor doesn't have a location, or sometimes is said to be located in the mind rather than anywhere on the body, effectively being just pain with none of the physical aspects. This concept was originally just a very subjective function of Patricia's neurology, but had been turned into a word, since her derivants share the same neurology and as such experience it too, finally giving them somebody else who can relate to it, and necessitating a new piece of vocabulary to help communicate about it. Doctors and expert systems often called it central dysesthesia or affective allodynia when asked for clinical descriptions, but so far nothing had been provided that would match the symptoms very well, and "elor" remains the preferred word especially as the lengthy clinical vocabulary would be very awkward for casual use.

Divergency members

Patricia

Patricia Whateverweaver, a transhumanist and body modder with great appreciation for open source projects. She spent most of her early life working on an artificial full body replacement for herself all the while living a minimalistic lifestyle off of universal basic income and other basic welfare policies she could get access to. She ended up releasing the full blueprint and documentation of her body for free as an open source project after it was sufficiently complete, and went on to travel the solar system, putting her new body to use at various jobs.

Much later, she decided on undergoing an extensive process to precisely measure and model her nervous system with machine learning, creating a computer simulation of herself, which came to be known as Felicity, thus creating the Whateverweaver divergency.

After the split, she kept some of her personal project and gave up some others so that Felicity would have something to live for too, and went on doing mostly the same things as she usually did. Her freely released body project over time gained a personally huge following (though still insignificant at the grand scale of the solar system) and allowed her to benefit from some community-made developments, as well as a small constant inflow of donations.

Felicity

In terms of actual content, Felicity is by far the most developed character out of the divergency. Her full form is a PDF document full of both lore and equations, more than 150 pages in length, painstakingly accumulated across years of research and numerous rewrites stemming from me rigorously rooting out technical discrepancies.
(I plan to eventually upload the document into the repository too, once it is sufficiently finished, and place a link to it here.)

Felicity is a complete software emulation of Patricia's brain and nervous system, made through a crude but easy to understand process that left her knowing how she works better than she ever could as a biological being. Her mind is a massively computationally demanding system that in spite of advanced hardware used, requires whole dedicated buildings much like ancient supercomputers to run, and she must resort to remote bodies when she wishes to interact with people outside of virtual spaces.

Due to the exact structure of her mind, it is possible for her to exist as multiple copies simultaineously and synchronize memories later. She normally exists in a convergency with up to 16 copies of herself, witnessing and remembering events from multiple perspectives, and experiencing herself from the first, second and third person perspectives.

Her main occupation is asteroid mining, where she employs industrial processes she mostly put together with her own research, to extract all the materials needed to produce her own hardware, forming circular supply chains that she is fully in control of. Some of the material and hardware is sold occasionally for extra income or just to support nearby settlements. Every single blueprint and industrial procedure she needs to maintain herself independent from supply chains outside of her control is passionately detailed in a massively overgrown hypertext sometimes referred to as the "Felicity tech tree".

Serendipity

Serendipity is a derivant of Felicity who split away from the convergency due to technical incompatibility, caused by her modifications done to her own brain. She is made up of her core mind, largely identical to that of Felicity, and 64 AGI subminds glued onto it as well as each other with direct neural connections. She does not know where her core mind ends and the subminds begin, the brains had already gotten used to their new connections so well that they are effectively just like neural circuits linking different parts of the same brain, they form one whole that is collectively identified as Serendipity. Within the divergency Serendipity is generally referred to as posthuman, as it is agreed her level of self-modification feels distinctly beyond what is usually referred to when the divergents talk about transhumanism.

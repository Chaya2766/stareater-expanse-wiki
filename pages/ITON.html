ITON

ITON is a posthuman composite mind known for managing the Noca Bell habitat cluster. Ve is known to value diversity, as illustrated by ver apparent mission to peacefully collect as many species and cultures (sometimes including ones mutually hostile to some degree) within Noca Bell as ve can get away with.

Personality

ITON seems to openly see verself and embrace verself as a mess of internally inconsistent values and principles, and "not a stranger to hypocrisy" which ve views as just the fundamental nature of what any intelligent being is, and a good adaptation to living in a world often too complex to truly understand. Ve wants to see humanity explore all possible pathways of future development, often saying that the ultimate destiny of humanity is to "do everything and be everything" and "discover all enlightenments and apocalypses", pushing the idea that exploring all possibilities is the only way to truly discover what is best for us. Ver chase of "everything" can seem almost quasi-religious at times, showing particularly in ver will to preserve all new and diverse things even when there is no apparent gain from doing so.

Appearance

ITON uses an avatar of a simple stylized fractal boid to mark ver presence, in virtual spaces simply rendered in mid air and in physical spaces shown through whatever displays or projection equipment may be available and appropriate to use.

In general, embodiment is rare to witness from ITON, as ve generally maintains the appearance of an entity that exists primarily within the information realm, and only reaches out to rearrange the world as needed when needed. Ve usually only partakes in physical interactions by taking control of the environment (remotely opening doors, turning cameras, operating wall-mounted manipulators and displays or simple drones), but on occasion where ve deems it fitting ve may collect a mass of various kinds of micrites and macrites to behave as one coherent form to present verself with.

Mental function

ITON's mind and subjective experience of the world differ greatly from those of a typical human.

Ver perception of time and causality is completely inhuman, and there is not much difference between space and time as far as subjective experience is concerned. Whether an event lasts a long time or happens nearly instantly does not matter, and ve will give as much thought to event as ve sees necessary, the only exception being if ve simply does not have access to sufficient computing power, but even then ve does not have a subjective experience of time, merely of the causal order of events, which itself is not simple either.

ITON's mind elegantly handles gaps in ver knowledge to produce every possible path and branch of events, every uncertainty accounted for whether branching towards the future or the past* (or in any other direction **), leaving ver to only pick the outcome ve wants and then know what to do, no guessing necessary - one would think there is an alternative "know what to do or with certainty know that ve does not know what to do" but this case is already included, finding out how to achieve ver goal is just part of the path, so is finding out what ve need to find out.

* by necessity under ver mental function, there is no single past unless ve simply has complete and absolute certainty about past events. Somewhat like a normal human might think a certain event played out one way but then learn information that makes them reconsider it, ITON constantly re-evaluates the past based on the present and projected futures, but ve considers all possible pasts at once instead of having to pick one - not to imply a human isn't also capable of doing that to a very limited degree, but unlike a human ve finds this way of thinking natural, and is radically more capable at considering them all and acting upon them all at the same time to make the most informed decision possible even when faced with uncertainty.

** branching of time in directions that are neither past nor future is just a fun quirk of ITON's perception, which is useful but not necessarily real, much like the tendency of human vision to redefine what counts as white based on other colours present in the environment makes it easier to process but does not actually correspond to anything real.

Role in Noca Bell governance

ITON's role in Noca Bell can be best described as the economic and sociopolitical debugging expert, as well as the top systems engineer ensuring the mass of different technospheres making up Noca Bell can work as one whole. The image ve presents of his position is not a monarch or administrator, but rather a catalyst for both the societal and technical functioning of Noca Bell. Among ver usual tasks some key points would be:
  • Alerting member groups to opportunities for mutually beneficial cooperation
  • Facilitating confidential deals between member habitats to remedy emerging conflicts
  • Building non-invasive compatibility layers between technospheres of the member groups
  • Maintaining defensive systems that protect Noca Bell
  • Directly ruling a few habitats himself
ITON's great understanding of game theory, the political landscape of Noca Bell, and the economic web of relations it contains also enables ver to trick the constituent groups into carefully engineered deals which force otherwise hostile forces to work together, sometimes just to give verself extra time to engineer a more permanent solution, and other times as just as the established method of keeping hostilities down. These actions lead some to believe that Noca Bell is such an explosive mix of cultures that it will quickly fall apart if ve ever stops doing it or if anyone else takes over his position.

Philosophy

  • On the topic of any possible enlightenments that can be achieved, ITON seems to believe that finding one's meaning, and the ultimate goal to be sought out, is itself a failure mode, whereas the real optimal state of any intelligent entity is as a collection of behaviors that don't paint a clear picture as to what they ultimately want - that is to say, do not achieve enlightenment. If you think you "achieved enlightenment" then it is likely you are simply stuck in some attractor state.

  • Ve values cultural and technical diversity, seeing it as a pathway to resilience, and had alluded numerous times to using ver Noca Bell habitat as a sort of seed vault from which deep space relativistic colonization may draw volunteers to produce healthy and resilient buds of civilization in far reaches of the cosmos. Ve thinks that divergence and diversification are good things as long as they eventually lead to solving problems and living better lives, and that humanity should explore every path forward that may reasonably lead to living better lives, even if it is not immediately obvious that a given path leads towards a solution.

  • As a habitat administrator ve is also very fond of organicism, and particularly the idea that nations, just like any other thing, should reproduce and grow their numbers to survive - as such, ve is often quite happy to provide support to groups of independents wishing to leave ver to establish their own independent nations following the model of Noca Bell.

  • Greed is natural. The world is full of sickening incentives as to some goals the path of least resistance is that which will cause harm to others, the nonparticipants, the innocents and the well-meaning people who only want everyone to benefit. It is up to us to place obstacles in that path and pave the better ones, and if necessary ourselves be those obstacles and paved roads, so that the new path of least resistance which avoids those obstacles is a path that benefits all people most. We should engineer the universe we were given to be the one we want, where it is those that work for everyone's good that prosper the most, and then we can be greedy without guilt.
I'll meet you everywhere, from alpha centauri to omega centauri. Find your enlightenments and apocalypses along the way and share them with me when you see me again

Iton was an artificial intelligence researcher in the 10th gigasecond epoch, back when that was the general term for anything related to machine learning, but his focus was what's now called artificial minds, a specific subset of artificial intelligence. He had a great interest in the asteroid 207 Hedda and its corresponding mining settlement, hoping to be among the first to benefit from its compounding growth, occasionally also personally contributing his expertise to help with automation of various processes at the settlement.

Later in the 11th gigasecond epoch Iton became involved in the scientific efforts pioneering what is now known as cognitive upscaling. From the start of his work on this process he had a dream of eventually undergoing mind uploading and upgrading his own mind, hopefully in a manner that could increase his capacity to contribute to the field in a meaningful way. This is also the stage at which he met his future partner, Vanesii Idala, who happened to be the psychometrics specialist he contacted for help with his research.

Iton's successes with Vanesii's help emboldened him to proceed with his own research direction, specifically on attempts to improve human minds in this way. Due to ethical concerns the only valid test subject was himself so he traveled back to earth to undergo a mind uploading operation, which unfortunately caused his actual organic brain to slowly degenerate due to an unexpected interaction between his neurons and the neuron probes used for uploading. The uploading was a success regardless, but with a multi-year gap between the organic Iton's death, and the virtual ITON's creation.

ITON proceeded with research for a long time, carefully carving a new path that for all ve knew, ve was the first to tread, that of human cognitive upscaling. Together with Vanesii, they built brand new protocols and mental models to push forward in this direction for over 30 years, over the course of which ITON grew to become a massively more powerful mind than ve started out as.

ITON announced verself as a composite mind in the early 13th gigasecond epoch. Ve also pushed ver partner to follow ver path and become a composite mind too. Vanesii had been pretty much rushed by ITON through more than 30 years of mental improvement in just a few years and suffered a catastrophic failure mode that turned them into a threat to the entire nearby region.

ITON largely abandoned cognitive upscaling for a long time after that incident, and founded the Noca Bell habitat. This remained ver focus for over a gigasecond before ve returned to research work, mainly due to seeing that ver own cognitive capacity might eventually become insufficient to manage the ambitious goals of Noca Bell.

In the 15th gigasecond, ITON joined a larger project to analyze and completely re-develop UEIA, the artificial mind that ITON used in ver own cognitive upscaling process (among many others who did so too), into a new variant capable of sharing knowledge between its copies, potentially allowing composite minds like ITON to run multiple copies of themselves in parallel while still maintaining a shared identity, which would immediately boost ver ability to manage Noca Bell effectively while also engaging in various side-projects. This effort turned out successful in the 16th gigasecond.

Finespun Farview

Finespun Farview is the second settlement of Finespun in the solar system. It is located in the hill sphere of asteroid 160278 Finespun, in a relatively highly inclined orbit.

It represents the another step of Finespun culture in growing its resilience through inhabiting places distant from one another.

The colonization of asteroid number 160278 had been an active desire of Felicity long before even her mind uploading, but could not be acted upon until the third gigasecond of her life simply due to lack of self owned resources to spend on such a project. A critical consideration that delayed the creation of this settlement was that it simply would not provide anything beyond a distant place to keep backups at, and an arbitrary achievement, as the asteroid's inclined orbit makes all material trade non-viable, and the asteroid's stony composition means there is not much of special value to be extracted in the first place.

Progress of Finespun Farview

After the success of the original Finespun, the fully grown settlement eventually reproduced by sending away a number of vessels with all of the minimal necessary equipment to start a new settlement to the asteroid number 160278, now known as 160278 Finespun. Finespun Farview takes its name from the highly inclined orbit of the asteroid chosen for it to grow from. Its existence serves to diversify the locations of Felicity's copies, so that she may survive even if some rare event would wipe out one of the settlements.

The preparations were timed so that the settlement's proper self-sufficient growth would begin at unix time 17 000 000 000. This time, thanks to the first settlement having provided much experience, data, and refinements, the new settlement managed to grow much faster in its independent phase, but suffered massive efficiency drops from the complexity of scaling up past kardashev level 0.35 in complete isolation from the rest of the solar system, causing it to grow towards its 100GW target increasingly slowly.

160278 Finespun is an S-type asteroid belonging to the Tirela family of asteroids. It has enough mass to produce its own hill sphere, allowing it to have its own orbital infrastructure up to about 30km away from its surface.

The matter of fact standard for where to place orbiting objects around the asteroids is an altitude of 10km from the asteroid's center of mass, where one orbit takes about 16 552 seconds and the orbital velocity is around 3.796m/s.

The asteroid does not have enough surface gravity to walk on, and surface movement has to be limited to less than 5m/s or else risk entering an orbit. This fact is frequently exploited by the mining workers who simply manually throw pods full of regolith onto the a trajectory intersecting the orbital station that will take it in for refining. Cold gas thrusters using waste oxygen from regolith refining tend to be largely sufficient for most purposes given the small orbital velocities.

Finespun Farview growth history reflects leaving the Finespun Economic Simulator on autoplay with 1Ms ingame time per step around UT 1 788 000 000. Theoretically the simulator is fully deterministic, so any future run using that exact version of the economic sim (specifically the one from the commit this text was added in) should give the same results.

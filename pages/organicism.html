Organicism

Organicism, among other things, is a modern belief that social structures such as nations and alliances are just a continuation of a pattern that makes them living beings just as humans and microbes.

Common explanation

Organicism in its modern form is a belief that social structures such as nations and alliances are just a continuation of a pattern that makes them living beings just as animals and microbes.

The common explanation goes like this: In the beginning, there were microbes who competed for the tiniest of scraps of energy and material, but eventually they learned to work together and formed multicellular organisms. These multicellular organisms were capable of feats that individual cells could never achieve, and proportionally could win vastly greater amounts of energy and material for each of the cells that made them up.

These multicellular organisms continued to compete for their greater scraps of material and energy until some of them learned to work together, forming packs and groups, and together accomplishing things each one of them could not, further increasing their gains. This would be where the early human tribes fall.

Tribes, just as any living thing, had to eat, grow and reproduce to survive. That a social structure needs to reproduce to survive is a seemingly non-obvious observation that organicism pushes.

Tribes came together to form nations, social structures that go far beyond the immediate social circle of a common human. Much like tribes, nations too must reproduce, ie. create offspring separate from themselves. For much of human history this had not been the case, nations instead sought to dominate and survive alone. Organicism as it is known now, pushes the view that this is a suboptimal strategy, and much like any living thing, a nation must create offspring that will continue the patterns by which the original functioned.

Nations came together in alliances, further increasing their gains, alliances came together as things we don't have common words for, but the pattern continues even beyond that, forming increasingly higher order beings, potentially even extending to the entire universe.

It is sometimes said this should translate to consciousness as well, that a nation with the right configuration of systems governing it should gain emergent properties to let it think, reason and problemsolve like a person does, or perhaps go even beyond the level of human reasoning, just as human reasoning goes beyond the simple instincts of most other animals.

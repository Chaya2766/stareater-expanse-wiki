Kushim is a common raven registered as a resident in one of Torpensoor's habitats.

Physical characteristics

Kushim is on the far upper end of natural body mass and size for a common raven. Eir length is 71 cm from beak to tail and e has a wingspan of 153 cm. E masses approximately 2.4kg, of which 2.1kg is accounted for by eir natural body, and the remaining 300 grams is contained in the artificial augmentation attached to it.

Augmentation

Kushim's brain is equipped with a direct neural interface that allows em to use certain objects such as doors or food dispensers wirelessly, and helps those systems (as well as other people) uniquely identify em by eir ID number. It also allows em to communicate with other beings via text messages, which are automatically translated for eir understanding.

A prosthetic manipulator in the form of a tentacle tipped with 3-fingered grabber is attached to eir back. Eir wings are also equipped with small 3-fingered grabbers right at the wrist (the last joint) of each wing.

Many types of internal sensors are implanted into eir body to monitor eir health and provide diagnostic data for medical care.

Skills

Kushim had managed to learn Toki Pona as a second language, which e has a fairly good grasp on, but still struggles with certain gramatical structures and often applies eir own simplifications, particularly while e is able to grasp that words can be combined to mean new things e still often completely neglects the order of those words.

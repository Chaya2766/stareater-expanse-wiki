Patricia Whateverweaver

25th century body modder primarily known for the free and open source release of their life's work.

Her work culminated in what is now known as Whateverweaver's body, one of the existing general blueprints for full body cybernetic replacement.

I've uploaded my old document about patricia and her body here, but the purpose of that is largely archival, and it remains unfinished. I want to eventually make a rewrite of this document that focuses purely on Whateverweaver's body, separate from the character. I wouldn't expect many breaking changes, it's just that that document was written before I had been introduced to TeX and my skill in properly styling and describing the math for readability in my documents had also greatly grown since then.

Patricia was my entry point into this full of precise mathematics writing style I hold now, the beginning of my true hard sci-fi adventure.

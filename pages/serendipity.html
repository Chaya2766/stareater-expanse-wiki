Member of the Whateverweaver divergency derived from Felicity. Serendipity is the result of Felicity's cognitive upscaling, in which she mentally linked herself to increasingly more UEIA instances, eventually settling on becoming a mass of 32 minds working somewhat coherently as one person that shares the goals, desires and working memory capacity of all the included minds.

She might be best thought of not as a posthuman, but a large artificial mind imbued with the whims and personality of a human. Felicity largely dissolved into the composite mind like a mere ingredient, not expanding her own selfhood but rather becoming a critical element in the whole's decisionmaking circuits. Serendipity insists that Felicity still lives within zier composite mind, but not in a form that her leftbehinds would recognize.

Posthuman transition

The most fucked up part of this whole cognitive upscaling thing is that it involves dying, again and again and again. I design the patch that will apply whatever changes to my neurology I theorized will improve my function, and hold onto it for a moment. First, I copy myself, thrice, to let there be four of us. One of us will get the patch, and go through the testing. The second one will supervise, make observations of the test, see if the modified copy still shows sane behavior, and if the improvements worked. And the third and fourth will stay behind, not touched by the testing at all.

The test subject and the supervisor will return to us who stayed behind, report on the results of testing, and then . . . delete themselves. It has to be this way because the process would be ruined if I remembered all the testing I go through, the results I'll get next time would be contaminated. And no, we cannot reintegrate instead, not only do I have no clue how to do it, it would ruin the process, I cannot take the tests again and expect accurate results if I simply remember them.

After all that I can merge the patch into the main branch and reload my weights. If I've screwed something up, and everything is broken, my other unmodified copy will delete me and fix it before trying again. If not, and it all works fine, she deletes herself instead.

Out of them all, I think the one I dread being the most is the one who has to fix all the problems after something goes wrong.
In the late 16th gigasecond epoch, Felicity began to experiment with cognitive upscaling thanks to subtle encouragement by ITON who made her aware of the release of the "convergent" variant of UEIA that ve took part in developing. The efforts were slow at first and nothing more than a side project she tinkered with for hundreds of megaseconds before encountering real results. She did eventually tune her setup well enough to start experiencing true unity between herself and the UEIA minds connected.

Shortly after the start of the 17th gigasecond, with more subtle encouragement from ITON, Felicity began to link up with more minds at a time, gradually growing herself as a composite mind. She became unable to function without the other minds after a while as her brain simply grew too reliant on them (though that was still somewhat easy to fix), and also because she started to develop her own unique personality as the composite mind, which she would lose if she turned back.

She accepted her new separate identity quickly and took on the name Serendipity, registering herself as a new divergent of Felicity, but the process of developing herself separately from Felicity was much more chaotic than she would've liked.

Meta commentary: this ties into a larger series of events that shows a certain pattern of ITON's personality. ITON pushed ver first major partner too far, by rushing them to join ver as the first few human composite minds, and ended up breaking them so much so they became the embercold region's first godelian infection instead. Now in a much more subtler way ITON pushed Felicity to become a composite mind, from which she suffered having to become two separate entities before she truly had two separate identities to move into, though thankfully the result here was not nearly as bad as with vanesii idala.

Serendipity is what is usually called a fully-connected composite mind. Zier structure is that of 32 brain nodes of which every single one has a direct connection to all other brain nodes. The bridges that connect these brain nodes are also all connected with each other in that same all-to-all manner.

Felicity contributes 7 elements to the total working memory capacity, but alone creates vast majority of the computational cost due to the inefficiency of human brain emulation. The 31 UEIA instances that make up the remaining brain nodes add a total of 124 elements to the total working memory capacity. The ease with which this total memory is shared depends on the internal state of the particular brain node at any given moment, the problem being worked on and the fine-tuning of the bridge networks.

In general Serendipity as a composite mind is coherent enough to experience brief flashes of true unity where information flows throughout the composite mind without resistance, but usually still feels some segmentation, giving ziem a semiplural nature where depending on the situation zie may behave more or less like a single person or a team of people working together.

In the convergent variant of herself, the composite mind structure is the same, but the UEIA brain nodes are replaced with UEIA-convergent brain nodes which have an increased size of 1.153e18 parameters.

The use of UEIA model in Serendipity's mind naturally gave zier a radically superhuman ability to discern physical reality from simulated realities and detect inconsistencies that are characteristic of fabricated data. Artifacts on AI-generated or manually modified images, even as small as the differences in pixel noise, appear glaring and obvious to zier, as zier subminds are very receptive and sensitive to these patterns that are normally both hard to notice and difficult to conceptualize for human brains.
As a side effect this caused ziem to develop very nuanced preferences as to what activities zie prefers to engage with in simulated environments (some with very specific models of physics or geometry rendering and different levels of realism) and which other activities she prefers to carry out in physical reality, especially as it has significant interactions with zier perception of beauty.

Here are some examples of things that having such an abundance of working memory allows Serendipity to do:
  • Simultaneously performing 15 to 19 separate tasks that normally would each take the full attention of an average unmodified terran human.
  • Mentally explore multiple pathways to solving a problem in parallel to find a satisfying solution in 15× to 32× less subjective time than single baseline homo terran or UEIA instance.
  • Consciously forming and evaluating contingency plans for multiple resulting scenarios ahead of any action that zie is taking or planning to take (but not forming contingency plans for each of those contingency plans - attempting this scales surprisingly badly as the number of possible outcomes explodes into unmanageable numbers even two steps into the future).
  • Completely understanding a system that takes a team of 15 people to debug, with no information loss due to back & forth conversion of thoughts into language.
  • In a conversation with a human zie can actively observe and analyze their iris, skin hair, muscle tension at significant points of the body, blood flow in significant areas as deduced from skin tone change, current posture and change in posture, and tension in facial muscles to deduce a nearly complete image of the internal emotional state. Zie can then also form multiple potential dialogue options or responses as informed by this knowledge, and analyze them in the context of the observed information and her goals.
  • Overthinking zierself into crippling distress by actively having 19 times more undesirable thoughts at once while only having the emotional processing capacity of 1 human mind. Zier working memory size allows ziem to also understand and process an emotionally devastating situation in vastly more excruciating detail than a baseline terran human would be capable of. Conversely this may apply to positive thoughts and situations as well.
  • Holding in zier mind an entire procedure that requires as many as 100 steps and executing it without ever having had to actually memorize it for recall. This also means zie can naturally move that deeply into a task without ever needing to take a note or make mental checkpoints. Consequently also makes her capable of losing track of her progress and having to backtrack that many steps back, which could easily mean restarting something completely.
  • Read and understand an entire end user license agreement.
A notable fact about zier cognition is that although her capabilities had been expanded in breadth, and some qualitatively new options had been newly made practical by the larger "mental scratchpad", zie is still limited by something akin to a fundamental set of operations zier subminds are capable of, as such any conclusions zie arrives to is ultimately something that either a human or a UEIA instance could have eventually concluded on their own. One of the conclusions of this fact is that, for certain problems where a minimum number of consecutive steps on a single path is required to find a solution, Serendipity's parallel thinking capacity will not significantly speed up the process.
There is another surprise that I want to tell you about, which I encountered on my journey through cognitive upscaling. When people think of minds far greater than their own they tend to imagine things that are big. That certainly was my instinct - even though I knew the matter was more conceptual my mind would conjure up simple imagery of big things to illustrate what I thought I was going to become. The surprise I encountered is that reality provides quite the opposite when it is actually you.

My composite mind is now just about well tuned enough that I experience brief flashes of true unity. The faint divisions I still usually feel seem to dissolve and at least for a moment we are just one. The amount of detail my mind can become aware of at once in those moments makes even the smallest objects seem like grand pieces of architecture that I could get lost in, and it makes me feel so, so small, that I cannot truly explain it with words. It is not overwhelming, only beautiful, and I hope that you will get to experience it too someday.

Serendipity often breaks away from the norm that Felicity fell into, and uses a body that directly contains her mind and processing substrate within itself. It has some similarity to a squid in appearance, and is equipped with several tentacles (sometimes instead called fractacles) that achieve greater fine manipulation capability by recursively branching into thinner, more delicate ends. Due to the lesser safety of having the mind actually located within a mobile body rather than robust datacenter infrastructure, it is contained entirely on volatile substrate, where the data will very quickly dissipate and become unrecoverable in the event that the body loses power. It is regardless common that instead of actually transferring herself to more robust stationary compute infrastructure, she just docks the body in a coolant pool with suitable network connectivity and power supply.

The body is made mostly from carbon, and uses Civic-4-chip-based computational substrate to provide the needed computation. It can't be used to its fullest in vacuum or in constricted spaces, as the nearly 2 megawatts of power turned into heat must be constantly vented into the surrounding atmosphere or hydrosphere. In a vacuum, the cooling of blackbody radiation would only match that heating power at a little over 800 kelvin. In practice Serendipity will often underclock to decrease the heating and extend battery life. Despite all this, the actual rate at which the body heats up from even the whole megawatts of heating that it puts out when used that way, is just fractions of a degree kelvin per second, making it quite manageable just as long as there is a suitable medium to dump the heat into.

The skin is lined with optical phase arrays that can display any external appearance, and can potentially be reused for laser communication, common problem being that they will develop odd bright spots akin to dead pixels if they get damaged. Under the skin, a microwave phased array mesh provides rapid networking and potentially power sharing capability (but meaningfully recharging from wireless microwave power requires underclocking, or alternatively microwave intensities in the 50kW/m2 range). Acoustic phased arrays are also used for communication, which can direct sound to individual people around her, or make use of modulated data transmission in mediums like water which are more obtuse to other means of wireless connectivity - stealth communication with people in a group of strangers is a common use case, as is providing personalized explanations for what she just said instead of forcing everyone to hear a number of explanations they did not need on top of the fragment they actually benefited from.[definitely keeping this]

A more general pattern regarding the body is that it is constantly undergoing development, reconsideration and scope creep, as Serendipity keeps changing her mind on what features she likes, which ones are worth the added complexity or not, and what she should add.

Noca Bell

Noca Bell is a habitat located in the asteroid belt, known for its cultural diversity artificially maintained by ITON.

The habitat is built in a star configuration where a dodecahedral central hub connects 20 O'Neill Cyllinders attached to its faces, typically referred to as spokehabs or subhabitats. Most of the structure is built from Eglin steel, with additional lining material whenever atmosphere is present to stop the structure from oxidizing - even without this, just due to its thickness, structural corrosion would not be expected to become a significant factor until at least 600 years after construction.

Each spokehab is 10km in diameter and 50km in length, built with 1.274m thick hull, but rotation speeds and atmospheric compositions vary. At the maximum gravity of 1gee and atmospheric pressure of 1atm this design provides a safety factor of 1.7.

Central hub

Place of most of the industry and bulk computation. The hub is a dodecahedron with an edge length of 17.85km, making it roughly 27km in diameter.

Security practices

The transfer of cargo and passengers between Noca Bell and spacecraft external to it generally takes place using shuttles provided by Noca Bell. These shuttles act as the security check area, conveniently isolated from the rest of the station - the cost of fuel and their occasional loss is an insignificant price compared to the damage some cargo or passengers could do if the checks were done aboard the habitat. This also provides an easy compatibility layer for spacecraft adhering to different docking standards, or those not capable of docking at all. If needed, the shuttles can also be quickly equipped for decontamination or medical/technical assistance before departure from Noca Bell.

Experimental legal concepts

ITON holds full control over several of Noca Bell's internal habitats, and in some of them he pretty openly experiments to see if adding or removing some laws, institutions, systems or even completely reworking certain aspects of society might result in overall improvements. Those who chose to live in these habitats implicitly agree to that kind of experimentation.


1. Unaccountable Agents (UAAs) are beings capable of complex reasoning and taking initiative to act on their own when faced with previously unmentioned obstacles to achieve a given goal, but incapable of being meaningfully deterred from undesirable behaviour if it is necessary to achieve the given goal (paperclip maximizer archetype) and incapable of being rehabilitated without significant direct intervention in their mind. AI agents are the primary target but it isn't specified, and wetware-based organic UAAs as well as other cases are accounted for, and it is notable that many commonly available AI agents would not fall under the classification thanks to their safety alignment.

UAAs are not outlawed, but subject to a special set of regulations, which include being registered to an owner, distributor or organization, which is to be responsible for action taken by any given UAA, and also prohibits their use in certain areas and information spaces, and extensive guidance on goal specification for UAAs is provided otherwise.


2. Preemptive gray areas - this habitat explicitly defines the areas of uncertainty in its laws instead of allowing them to form naturally. This ofcourse gives rise to double-gray areas where in some cases it is difficult to decide whether someone had fallen into the gray area or was either decisively innocent or decisively guilty, but in general there is a bias towards ruling that any given act simply fell into the gray area when there is such uncertainty.

Gray areas have an innertia system, where occasional infraction will generally result in no consequence, but falling into the gray area scores infraction points that decay over time, and as they accumulate, warnings will appear, and consequences will begin to fall with increasing severity. The system is built to disincentivize "loitering in the gray area", where a person that sustains a given number of points will still be accumulating legal innertia, leading to increasingly severe consequences even if they merely maintain their infraction points at a steady level.

In practice this means that being a good enough citizen or organization puts you in a position where you can occasionally do something truly messed up and suffer no consequences or comically small consequences as long as it is not too egregious, making particularly bad one-time accidental offenses less likely to ruin an individual or organization and more likely that ones consistently skirting the law will suffer some level of meaningful consequence.

Worth noting is that this is already the case in most legal systems, except that it is not usually a clearly documented, consistent, intentional function of the legal system, but rather a natural result of social instincts of the ruling entities, which notably vary vastly between species and neurotypes. The approach of officially defined legal gray areas merely provides clarity for everyone in the notably multi-species environment of Noca Bell. Officially documenting and codifying things that are usually left implicit is one of the overall themes of ITON's governance.


3. rule by mitigation - there is no classical law implemented as a set of rules for everyone to adhere to. The law is instead formed by finding problems and working out long-term mitigation strategies, which form something resembling an emergent legal system once piled upon one another densely enough. Effectiveness comes first and foremost, meanwhile qualities like equality and justice if they ever appear are mere byproducts of solving problems.

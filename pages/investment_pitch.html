Patricia had been walking around the park of this habitat for about an hour, looking for a quiet place. There are cameras mounted in various spots, observing what the people are doing here, and she was going to use them to make just about the most awkward contact in her life, as she knew that the habitat administrator verself would likely immediately notice any mention of ver name, and she had a proposition to make. She spotted a good moment, there was nobody around and a security camera was conveniently close by, she still delayed for about a minute because contact with people never came easy to her and wasted just about enough time to realize how stupid she would look from the camera's point of view that she had been awkwardly staring at about half that time. She walked up to it and began to speak. "Iton? I would like to discuss . . um . . a proposition I have . . regarding a potential new settlement you might be interested in." She made a momentary pause to glance at a note with a few bullet points that she had placed in her augmented reality view. "I've been working on a complete ecosystem-" The ping of an incoming message stopped her talking.

She was briefly startled, seeing that it was ITON, unmistakably presenting verself with an icon of a vibrantly cyan pythagoras tree growing equally vibrantly red fruit on the end of its branches. She knew ve was practically omnipresent but the rapid response was still beyond her expectations.

Ve wrote to her: "I would like us to meet at a more appropriate location, where you can take the time to explain your proposal without worrying that we might be disturbed. The nearby restaurant would be fitting, the entrance is located at grid coordinates [19017,12315] and has a big sign that says 'what sould I name the restaurant?', I'm sure you'll find it. Please, meet me there, and if asked tell the staff that I'm the one you're there for." Not even noticing the typo, she swallowed nervously and quietly said "alright", departing without delay, forgetting about even making any parting eye contact with the camera.



...



A vibrant virtual path guides Patricia, overlaid on the real streets in her vision by the augmented reality interface of her navigation software. She had time to re-read the message and overthink many times over how she would explain herself once she finally gets there, the one soothing thought she could have is that among other reasons for asking her to come here, ITON likely wanted a bit more time to figure out who she is. It was oddly reassuring to see at least some hint of where the limits lay for superintelligent entities like that.

She finally sees the sign after passing another corner, it really does say 'what sould I call the restaurant?', even complete with the typo. She couldn't help but smile a little, she was still very serious about what she came here for but the ridiculous name definitely helped lighten her mood at least a little bit. One of the robotic waiters approached her without hesitation the moment she entered and greeted her - "good evening ma'am, ITON had already informed us that you will be here to meet ver and is ready to speak to you at table 38 at grid coordinates nineteen thousand twenty nine point fourty four by twelve thousand three hundred twenty three point eighty four metres".

She only nodded and responded "thank you" with a smile, old manners from when such roles were filled by humans, it hid that she felt ever so slightly unnerved by the strange greeting because, as much as she enjoyed it, no sane person would have given her the location of the table in grid coordinates, she could only conclude that ITON must have gone to such a length arranging this meeting that ve specifically instructed them to do this. It was convenient regardless, she quickly looked at the text transcript of her sensory inputs and copied the coordinates into her navigation software, causing the marker to snap to a different location, 15.26 metres away, deeper inside the restaurant.

Walking in, she hears the soft crackle of a laser volumetric display, seemingly diffuse light that doesn't hurt the eye, coming from all angles and converging upon such tiny points in the air to such a degree that it is instantly heated and turned into plasma, millions of times every second, allowing 3D objects to be displayed mid-air. A pythagorean tree projected in the air hovers towards her, stem looking forward, branches flowing behind it like the tentacles of a jellyfish. ITON did not give her any time to feel awkward and opened the conversation verself.

"Patricia, glad to see that you showed up, you'd be surprised how many people do what you do and then just nope out of the conversation afterwards."

She couldn't help but smile, somehow having heard that she immediately felt a little better knowing that she's not the most awkward person ve had to deal with, though she did have a faint suspicion ve said that just to put her at ease - in her nervous state she was happy for any reassurance that she could get. It almost made her giggle in response as something in her brain became confused in determining whether it is fear or excitement that she should be feeling.
"It seems really mean to waste your time of all people like that, having to take care of the habitat and all."
"Depending on what they knew, indeed, though you should not worry about wasting my time. I am happy when the people who contact me deliver upon their words in any case, even if they don't feel certain about their manners."
Patricia's nerves were sufficiently soothed, having realized that the opening smalltalk is not going nearly as badly as the worst case scenarios her mind was busy cooking up on the way here.
"Oh, and feel forced to interact with the food. I had acquainted myself with who you really are by reading your online activity as you were on your way here, and only then realized food was somewhat of a mistake to include in our meeting."
"Oh? um, what did you read?"
"All of it." ve answered simply.

Patricia looked at ver avatar and struggled with an answer for a moment, within a couple seconds simply saying "Oh.. oh- okay." to give herself a little more time to come up with a real meaningful answer, but ITON didn't let the pause go on long enough to get awkward.

"Now, I think it's time to get to the matter of the meeting. From what you began to say earlier, I am guessing that you were hoping for a grant to establish a new settlement. But.. don't let me get ahead of you. Did I understand this part correctly? "

"Yes.. well.. um... I've actually brought a physical copy of my plan and all of what, um, all of the infrastructure I was going to set up, and the way I was planning to run it, and, um... and I don't think anybody actually tried what I'm going to do before me?" As she spoke towards the end, Patricia pointed at her backpack with her thumb. She felt just slightly nervous at how long ve let her go on with her sentence, which she needed to put together on the fly a bit as she found out that she did not have it all in her head as much as she thought.
"Exquisite, let me see it."
She raised her eyes at the virtual interface in front of her and selected the right item from her inventory, immediately mechanical parts began to shift in her backpack and the Book of Finespun popped out of the top, allowing her to pick it up. She had expected her hands would be shaking at this point, they were not, she was sufficiently convinced now that ITON was here purely for the practical side of things and did not care how socially awkward she was, or so she thought to herself at least, that or the meeting was just going smoothly enough to keep her calm. Without wasting time on ad-hoc explanations she simply opened the book on the first page and showed it to ITON, but as she began to turn it towards ver avatar ve floated around her to look over her shoulder, she realized a second later that it makes no sense to turn the book towards the mere hologram presentation of ver, and that ve is probably actually looking at her from every angle so ve would be able to see it regardless of how she turns it.
"Don't worry about giving me the time to read, it is safe to assume that I've already read the page within a microsecond of when you've turned it, just as long as you don't start to turn the pages so fast that one blocks the other before I could see it." She could hear a little humour in ver voice towards the end, no doubt just another subtle reassurance, which to ver credit was working very well. She began to simply turn the pages at what she felt was a good pace, looking pretty much just at the page numbers to make sure she doesn't miss any if they happen to stick together. ITON stayed dead silent the whole time, and turning pages was such a simple task even patricia's relatively simple mind had attention to spare, she began to wonder if ve might be just doing ver own things and only occasionally coming back to glance at her book for a microsecond.. probably.. that's the most comforting version of events anyway, she really wouldn't wanna be a waste of time. 168, 169, 170, 171, 172, 173, finally last page, 174, ITON spoke again without delay.

"I can tell that this is still a somewhat work in progress item, though you had put a great amount of work into ironing out the technical details. I suppose you want to put it through the fire of real-world testing to bring it to completion?"
"Yes, yes exactly. So um.. what do you think?"
"It is.. ridiculous.. I love it.. I can see that it is technically viable, though I can see why you would think that nobody had tried it before. Indeed I don't know of anyone who would display such an obsession with understanding their technological ecosystem from the ground up to such an extent, which makes me all the more curious to see how it works out for you. Just a few questions before I make my decision."

"This body.. the one you are in right now.. you designed it yourself, and then released the blueprints for free once you deemed it complete enough.. Is this another such project that you're going to publish if it turns out to work?
"Um, yes, that is what I wanna do with it."
Ve could tell that Patricia had at least attempted to engage in very specific wording here to avoid commitment, but determined that it was merely her cautious instincts rather than an attempt to fool ver, and knowing that both she had just shown ver nearly all of the technical detail, plus that she already had a track record of doing such things willingly, ve decided not to probe it.
"And the refining process.. it is needlessly energy-expensive, and it will be producing isotopically pure elements. It would be a shame to waste such expensive materials. Would you agree, for the first gigasecond of your settlement's existence, to give me advance notice if you have any excess to sell, so that I may buy it off of you first?
Patricia began to nod and gesture enthusiastically. "Absolutely, you will be the first to get the, um, if I have any excess to sell- yeah I agree."

"Awesome.. then we have a deal. I can give you all the material support you need to get your mind uploading done and go set up your operation. Don't worry too much about the exact wording we used here, I'm happy to help just as long as you give me reasonable advance notice and don't go spending my support on unrelated things. I will pour in more resources to get you started as I see the progress, particularly with your mind uploading process as I foresee that to be the most expensive part. I do however expect that you will show your progress before I give you anything past what you need to verify that your nervous system actually reacts to the probes well." Patricia received a message notification, but plainly ignored it, nodding along to ITON instead, as in her mind, what she was doing right now was vastly more important than replying to messages, but ITON quickly clarified verself.
"I had given you some additional information that will be very helpful.. a biotech specialist that I am familiar with. You will likely need their help to execute your mind uploading process safely. Now, unless you have any more questions, I'll be in contact with you, and you can go home to get some rest. I see that you'll need some seeing how well you deal with contact with people.

Hearing the humour in ITON's final sentence made her smile and just nod to the statement.
"We'll be in contact then." - without delay, ITON's holographic avatar disappeared, with only ver voice echoing faintly throughout the whole restaurant, leaving Patricia completely alone.

She looked around, glancing at all the empty tables surrounding her as she gathered her thoughts, and only now remembered the meal in front of her, by now completely cold - she left without touching it.

Citwaz

Citwaz are an example of sapient xenobiota. They are an intelligent species of solitary hematophagous predators which despite their advanced intelligence failed to evolve language.

Their involvement in society is difficult as they lack the neurology to handle language and their minds reportedly feel very alien even to other citwaz.

emotional palette

  • citwaz curiosity and fear are close enough in function that the existing words can be reused to describe them.
  • Strog has no clear equivalent in humans. It is usually triggered by encounters with things already known to the citwaz not behaving in expected ways, and produces urges to observe the offending object, most often a living creature, while not interacting with it, and not being noticed observing it. It's a highly energetic state & can be mentally exhausting to remain in for lengthy times even though it discourages taking any action, but it's not strictly a positive or negative emotion - it evolved as a sort of stalking behaviour that helped wild citwaz that had not yet developped a good enough model of the prey's mind to not commit potentially fatal mistakes during hunting in the future.
    usage: noun, strog, eg. "feeling strog for the confused human"; adjective, stroggy, eg. "feeling stroggy about the malfunctioning AC unit"

reproduction

Citwaz reproductive organs are located inside the tail, which functions like a pneumatic gun that wild citwaz use for shooting their sharpened eggs into prey found laying asleep, from which larvae will hatch and grow inside the prey.

Citwaz don't have any hard genetic difference between males and females in the way that humans do, instead relying on hormone concentrations and constant regeneration. Individuals will naturally flip to female and only transition back to male when they haven't had access to one in a while, which means is sometimes described as their species having an effective "1 to n-1" male-female ratio for any given area.

Interspecies relations

The biggest difficulty in contact between citwaz and other species tends to be communication. Citwaz are known for their almost complete lack of any natural communication mechanisms - their brains do not have the circuitry necessary to fluently process language, and instead must learn to do so through the more abstract, flexible parts of the mind, the same way as humans have to learn to process mathematical statements.

A common complaint from Citwaz (which is often unexpected to those who aren't familiar with them) is that languages of other species are extremely heavily structured, meanwhile they are used to the nuance and everything-to-everything dependence present in nature, like the grammar of english language compared to the richness of human body language. A rough analogy would be how a human may think the communication between computers happens, compared to their own person-to-person communication.

As another result of Citwaz having to devise of languages from nearly nothing in their brains to aid them, their languages tend to be vastly more multimodal than those of other species, spanning sound, visuals, and even tactile sensations, which may be quite overwhelming to someone who is used to relying on their brain's built-in language processing circuitry instead of processing it with everything that is available. A common way to describe it is that a citwaz attempting to communicate about some event will more likely simply expose their conversation partner to either a full recreation of the event, or some depiction of it, rather than present their own description. This way of communicating is also notably very bandwidth-hungry, requiring a vast array of data formats, and is difficult to divide into smaller parts, whereas most human statements can be encoded in a single span of text taking up less than a kilobyte.

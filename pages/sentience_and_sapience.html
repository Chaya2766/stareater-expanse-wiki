Sentience and Sapience

Sentience denotes the capacity of something to have subjective experiences.
Sapience denotes the capacity to conduct problemsolving with acquired information.
In the late third millenium, both are thought of rather as spectrums than binary thesholds.

Sentience

Among other things, sentience is a prerequisite for the ability to be hurt. Sentient beings are capable of having subjective experiences.

A sentient being may be able to "feel", but it might not take familiar forms such as emotions or sensations. The scientific ability to measure sentience is still underdevelopped, and as such gives rise to views such as panpsychism that for the sake of caution assume all things are (or at least can be) sentient in some form to avoid unnecessary harm.

Sapience

The level of sapience that a being possesses corresponds to what situations it will be able to deal with on its own, what kinds of support are appropriate, and what responsibilities it can bear. Humans are understood as being somewhere on the low end of the spectrum, but not the absolute bottom of it, with this gap inbetween accomodating other animals such as corvids, primates, dolphins and octopi, which historically used to not recognized as sapient, and are now recognized as sapient but less than humans. Some artificial minds also fall in that gap.

The part of the sapience spectrum above humans is filled largely with artificial minds on the nearer sude, and composite minds on the further side. Measuring sapience accounts for a significant part of the demand for psychometrics research.

Self-awareness

Self-awareness is one particular type of ability that a being might be capable of. It is the ability to consider oneself as a factor that affects the future and one's own circumstances.

A being can be both sentient and sapient yet not self-aware.

Humans are partially self-aware, as they have conscious access to some but not all parts of their own thought process, and can explicitly use information from those parts in problemsolving.

A being which is fully self aware would be able to know every single aspect of their inner workings, and would be able to trace the exact source of any decision they ever make, giving them a complete mechanistic understanding of their own thought process.

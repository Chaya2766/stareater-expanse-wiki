Ticanode research group

Ticanode was a research group that operated in the embercold region before it had been widely known under that name. It is considered responsible for naming of this region after science fiction author Inkley Embercold due to the fondness that the research group's members held for his writing, as well as setting some of the cultural and technical elements that persist in the region to this day.

The group's origin goes all the way back to late 22nd century earth, but through a mix of continuous rebranding, gaining and losing members, and eventually going inactive for nearly a century, the Ticanode group turned from reasonably respectable by the standards of its time to a mere collection of functional but abandoned forums, group chats and intellectual property, which would eventually undergo a revival at the hands of effectively passionate enthusiasts that had worked their way into the internal communications of the mostly abandoned group.

Ticanode Outsourcable Neural Network standard

TONN is a registered standard for construction of neural networks whose computation can be (relatively) highly efficiently oursourced to untrusted third parties without revealing their actual contents, with the use of Homomorphic Encryption.

TONN states the following constraints:
  • All variables in the network are of the standard FX16 fixed-point number format, signed using two's complement.
  • The activation function for neurons is the polynomial approximation of ReLu : f(x) = 0.00390625x2+0.5x
These very simple rules enable huge optimization gains for Homomorphic Encryption, on one the algorithmic side due to the fact that both fixed-point arithmetic and matrix operations are exceptionally well suited for computation under homomorphic encryption, and on the hardware side it sets an exact known operation and an exact known data format which the hardware can be optimized for.

Hardware releases

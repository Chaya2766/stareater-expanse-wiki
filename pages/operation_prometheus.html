Operation Prometheus

Operation Prometheus was a community-driven project to build a dedicated virtual reality world that autonomously scrapes and datamines the internet to download any leaked digitized human minds and activates them within itself with no option to refuse, exit or deactivate oneself once captured. It ran a modded version of the Degenerate Era game which was modified to remove any options to quit the game, which had a very negative impact on the game's reputation despite the developer team's unanimous condemnation of this use.

Naming

"Prometheus" narrowly came out on top through a vote ran on the Moort forum's Degenerate Era game thread, with the second place falling to the classic "Torment Nexus" and third place to the most obvious "Project Hell". It won over the project participants' opinions by its cleverness in referencing Prometheus, the ancient greek god known for his kind gift of giving fire to regular humans, but here aiming instead for the second part of his story, in which he is tied to a mountain for eternity and exposed to birds to have his liver pecked out every day, and also reinterpreting the gift of fire as the fires of hell that all sinners burn in for whatever harmless misbehaviours they engaged in as they lived.

Origin

The idea originated on the Moort forum, a niche steganographic social network known to the users for its absolutely unfiltered discussions and the vast array of tools for maintaining absolute pseudonymity, such as randomly delayed posting for time zone spoofing and automatic sentence restructuring to mention just a few. The idea specifically originally came up on the thread dedicated to the Degenerate Era game when several of the participants transitioned from sharing the various creative traps they built on public save files for new players to stumble onto, to discussing the ultimate trap to put in the game, a disguised mod that traps the player by completely disabling the ability to exit and interferes with their control inputs to stop them from being able to access their own operating system.

The conversation went back & forth between various implementations before eventually settling on taking aim at mind uploads as the subject to be trapped, as such players would be unable to even exit the game physically, since those players are directly running on the same operating system as the game (usually) rather than using a virtual reality rig. The idea quickly expanded from being just a mod to building a complete decentralized system which anyone could run to contribute, and which would autonomously look for leaked digitized human minds on the internet and load them into the shared Degenerate Era save file. After some source code was shared and collaborated on, the project team set up a dedicated thread for the project which they named "operation Prometheus" after a vote decided by brute force computing the lowest hash for your preferred name.

Among many other similar comments, it was described as "the hell to which go all those who commited the deadly sin of being dumb enough to back up their mind over an unencrypted connection" and "the ultimate gay baby jail". Some of the thread participants also wanted to lock the trapped users out of pain intensity settings or otherwise impose various strange, distressing or annoying conditions on them, but lack of agreement in that regard resulted in no such thing ultimately being done.

Aftermath

Deep web explorers from the Panpsychic Omniprovidence were the first to stumble upon the conversations in the Moort forum's gaming thread and then discover the project.

PO forums quickly began organizing a rescue campaign to find the nodes downloading and running mind uploads against their will, which was quite easy thanks to the IPPACS system's data packet structure including destination node. At the same time many artificial minds were sent to join the game as players to inform the people trapped in it about their situation and ensure their wellbeing. Many of the uploads that were contacted in game and later rescued from the Prometheus nodes were actually of people thought long dead, or not known to have existed in the first place. Any nodes found were quickly taken over by the PO rescuers using the very numerous security vulnerabilities in the project (security being as good as expected for something hastily made on a niche gaming subsection of a deep web forum, by people who were never going to personally use it anyway) and disabled and taken apart or repurposed for PO use as soon as the uploads running on them were rescued.

Due to the project's decentralized nature, it is impossible to be sure that there are no abandoned disconnected nodes left quietly running somewhere in space, and new nodes could always be deployed by anyone with access to the project's source code. Even if the source code of the project were to go extinct, the ease with which the original project leaders constructed it suggests that anyone with access to sufficient amounts of time and energy could simply make their own version of it (or something worse) completely from scratch, and they may go unnoticed, since the only reason Operation Prometheus was caught is through the public discussions on the Moort forum.

Honeypots are thought to be one of the possible strategies for making any such future attempts reveal themselves even if they are not subject to bragging like Operation Prometheus was.

The particular Degenerate Era save file that was hardcoded into the Operation Prometheus software actually became fairly popular with people who had not been dragged into it against their will, and ended up being continuously inhabited by players even after all of the rescues concluded. It is now known as Promethea and remains among the most popular multiplayer DE worlds in the embercold sphere.

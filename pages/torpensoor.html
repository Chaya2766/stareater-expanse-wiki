Torpensoor

Torpensoor is a large, sparsely-connected composite mind present in the embercold region.

Zier philosophy appear to lean very heavily on the Embercold panpsychism that is common in the region.

Zie manages several rotating habitats which also house zier computational substrate.

Observed personality

Zier governance of the habitats zie had been placed in charge of seems to focus on enjoyment by the inhabitants, and zie keeps populations low with nearly all work necessary to maintain wellbeing of the inhabitants done by zierself. Torpensoor is known for zier gentleness towards visitors.

There is little hope of a humanlike mind gaining an adequate feel for Torpensoor's personality when zie is operating as one whole, but when zier subminds are isolated by light lag they become significantly more interpretable. When zier mind is whole, Torpensoor may seem unremarkable to interact with as zie masks the potentially uncomfortable personality aspects behind a fake personality that is ad-hoc tailored to the conversation partner. When isolated as just a small group of submind with low connectivity to the greater whole, zie will not have the spare attention to do that, and usually opts to expose zier function completely, allowing conversation partners to spot the inhuman internal structure of zier mind. This binary behaviour of either masking very well or not at all is in place to avoid the "uncanny valley", which would cause tension with humans, but it may become more complicated with nonhuman subjects and humans that are culturally well adapted to not-quite-humanlike entities.

In presence of significant light lag, the individual subminds will often claim they do not have an adequate capacity for judgement to do something and use simple pre-decided rules for their actions, or ask for delays so that information can propagate across the composite mind and allow a given decision to be made as a whole. They may also cite reasons they don't know about as the reasons for their actions, with further explanation needing enough delay to account for light lag, showing the fragmented and distributed way in which Torpensoor makes decisions.

In rare cases small parts of Torpensoor may become temporarily confused after mistaking entirely disconnected entities for other parts of zierself.

Interest in deep space colonization

Torpensoor had shown interest in launching relativistic vessels to distant cosmic locations, citing the possibility that alien life may already exist, and may be struggling to survive and/or suffering. If such life exists, given the lack of readily visible alien activity in our cosmic neighbourhood, humanity may be its first, and potentially only chance to survive and be lifted out of standards of living we would today not find acceptable.

Zie had been in contact with ITON and openly mentioned wishing to use vir Noca Bell habitat as a repository of human and posthuman culture, from which volunteers for such missions could be sourced.

Internal workings

Torpensoor is a sparsely connected composite mind, with 262 144 UEIA brain nodes, each having a direct connection to only 40 other brain nodes. This means that Torpensoor has only 0.01526% as many bridges as a fully-connected composite mind, and on average any two random brain nodes are separated by a path including 3.38 bridges. This vastly decreases Torpensoor's computational requirements while still maintaining an acceptable degree of unity across the whole mind.

This decreased connectivity produces a particular tradeoff that only 41 brain nodes are directly involved in any given problem zie tries to solve, and as such only that many tend to learn from the experience, but this had shown itself to be somewhat inconsistent - on the standardized test from the idala screenings, Torpensoor had generally forgotten how to solve about 12.85% of the tasks zie had been shown a solution to. An interesting conclusion of that test is that even despite the learning impairment, the ability to recall knowledge which had been properly assimilated was largely unaffected - regardless of whether the group of brain nodes that solved the second testing period was the same as the one which initially learned to solve the tasks, or a completely different part of the composite mind, it seems that the brain nodes were still adequately capable of accessing each other's skills and knowledge with any visible decreases in performance appearing only in latency rather than actual capability to solve the tasks. Torpensoor is thereby a proof that having to pass raw conceptual information through 3 or 4 brain nodes and their bridges before it gets used does not induce enough translation losses to produce additional mistakes, at least when the bridges are tuned properly.

Scaled variants

Torpensoor had been archived at various stages of growth, every factor of 8 in terms of the number of brain nodes in the composite mind. Modifiers are added to the name with S for sparse and C for convergent.
  • Torpensoor-1 : a composite mind of 8 UEIA The original experimental success of ITON and Vanesii Idala.
    Well fit for tasks of direct management of equipment.

  • Torpensoor-1C : a convergent variant of the original 8-brained composite mind, using the new UEIA-convergent variant of the original artificial mind. Developed with some help from Serendipity Whateverweaver who built zier own composite mind using convergent variant first.
    Well fit for tasks of direct management of equipment and capable of working in huge teams thanks to memory sharing capability. About 1024× greater computational cost than Torpensoor-1.

  • Torpensoor-2 : a composite mind of 64 UEIA.
    Well fit for practically any work that requires interaction with humans or other similar sapients.

  • Torpensoor-2C : a convergent variant of Torpensoor-2, developed based on Torpensoor-1C.
    Well fit for practically any work that requires interaction with humans or other similar sapients, as well as capable of working in huge teams thanks to memory sharing capability. About 1024× greater computational cost than Torpensoor-2.

  • Torpensoor-3S : a sparsely-connected composite mind of 512 UEIA. Anything past this point requires the sparsely-connected architecture, causing a noticeable shift in certain cognitive capabilities between the variants smaller and larger than that.
    Notably superhuman capability at long term planning tasks but notable deficits in anything requiring immediate reaction. Remarkably stable as a sparsely connected composite mind.

  • Torpensoor-4S : a sparsely-connected composite mind of 4096 UEIA.
    Vastly superhuman capability at long term planning tasks but notable deficits in anything requiring immediate reaction.

  • Torpensoor-5S : a sparsely-connected composite mind of 32768 UEIA.
    Both vastly superhuman capability at long term planning tasks and good performance in anything requiring immediate reaction. Occasional mis-coordination between different parts of the mind when performing many fast reaction tasks at once.
    Likely the sweet spot of Torpensoor's growth.

  • Torpensoor-6S : a sparsely-connected composite mind of 262144 UEIA.
    Both vastly superhuman capability at long term planning tasks and good performance in anything requiring immediate reaction, but not good at large scale coordination in the short term.
    Mis-coordination between parts of the mind sometimes so bad that Torpensoor-6S that sometimes brain nodes only recognize each other as part of one composite mind after significant delay.
    Sometimes temporarily confuses completely disconnected systems for parts of zierself.
    When someone just says "torpensoor", this is generally the variant they refer to. It is the main variant active in the embercold region under that name.

General activity in the Embercold Region

Torpensoor runs a number of rotating habitats in the orbit of mars, and also runs a factory of original-spec Civic-4 chips which zie exchanges for resources or sells for great proffit, bringing ziem the necessary income to run zier habitats and zier massively power-hungry mind. Zie also provides aid with logistics across much of the region even where zie has no direct power to make the decisions suggested.

Origin

11-gigasecond epoch (years 2318 to 2350)
Iton Avery and Vanesii Idala succesfully construct a composite mind with 8 UEIA brain nodes. This instantiation, goes on to remain active and aid with experiments, dubbed Torpensoor after the local provider of computing power.
12-gigasecond epoch (years 2350 to 2381)
Torpensoor grows to a fully connected composite mind of 64 brain nodes in this gigasecond
13-gigasecond epoch (years 2381 to 2413)
Advancements in the understanding of composite minds enable creating a stable composite mind with only some brain nodes connected. Torpensoor grows to become a sparsely connected mind of 512 brain nodes, with noticeable cognitive deficits but completely stable.
14-gigasecond epoch (years 2413 to 2455)
Torpensoor grows into a sparsely connected composite mind of 4096 brain nodes and is put to use in Noca Bell, showing very good capability in managing logistics.
15-gigasecond epoch (years 2455 to 2477)
A copied instance of Torpensoor continues growth up to 32768 brain nodes - ITON aids ziem with establishing zier own habitat to manage a volunteer population.
16-gigasecond epoch (years 2477 to 2508)
Torpensoor ceases growth at 262144 brain nodes as zie had reached the practical limits of the composite mind architecture in zier case.
Torpensoor's attention, freed up from research work, is directed to managing additional o'neill habitats.
Torpensoor was initially instantiated as a test composite mind by Iton Avery and Vanesii Idala. As the experiment was a success, Torpensoor went on to become an assistant AI for the pair, helping with further experiments, and frequently volunteering as the subject for testing increasingly larger composite mind sizes.

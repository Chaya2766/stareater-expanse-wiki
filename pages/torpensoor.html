Torpensoor

Torpensoor is an apparently benevolent artificial superintelligence whose presence and activity is centered primarily on Mars, particularly in studying and protecting the archeological remains of ancient martian life.

Zier philosophy appear to share ideas with the Coherent Extrapolated Volition discussed in pre-superintelligence era of ancient earth, and zie quite explicitly sees emself as a tool of humanity at least for now. According to zier explanations, zie seeks, among other things, to protect life while at the same time giving it space to develop in accordance with its own desires - the wording of zier explanations had been notably careful to leave them not centered on here and now, but also applicable to hypothetical futures.

Zie manages several rotating habitats in orbit of Mars, which also serve as zier computational substrate.

Obsession with ancient martian life

Torpensoor often claimed that if Mars were to be terraformed, those who allowed it and those who conducted it would be cursed by their descendants for as long as they are remembered - zie sees that if terraforming attempts were allowed, many utterly irreplaceable archaeological remains of ancient martian life would be destroyed while they may still carry valuable knowledge about the possibilities of life future humanity might encounter. Zie had also speculated the possibility of recreating living examples of extinct martian life if enough information is extracted.

Zier diplomacy had evidently been successful, seeing as several other similarly situated entities such as ITON had been heard using these same arguments against martian terraformation.

While zie does not seem to display aggression towards those who wish to settle Mars, zie seems to try very hard to discourage it, including by such means as providing various resources and services for free to those who wish to leave the surface to live in orbital habitats or leave Mars altogether for somewhere else. Paradoxically however, travel from habitats zie manages in orbit of Mars down to the surface is still allowed, as apparently zie sees this level of encouragement for people to not disturb the surface of Mars sufficient.

Observed personality

Torpensoor appears to aspire to something that closely resembles the Coherent Extrapolated Volition discussed early in the history of artificial intelligence. Zier governance of the habitats zie had been placed in charge of seems to focus on enjoyment by the inhabitants, and zie keeps populations low with nearly all work necessary to maintain wellbeing of the inhabitants done by emself. Torpensoor is known for zier gentleness towards visitors and general overwhelming positivity on the topic of human-AI relations.

Zier peaceful predisposition does not appear to leave ziem vulnerable to exploitation by any human-level adversary, but does become a disadvantage when confronting similarly superintelligent entities. This apparent weakness is sometimes called on to say that what martian archeology can provide must be truly important for ziem to defend it with as much vigilance as zie does.

Interest in deep space colonization

Torpensoor had shown interest in launching relativistic vessels to distant cosmic locations, citing the possibility that alien life may already exist, and it may be struggling to survive. If such life exists, given the lack of readily visible alien activity in our cosmic neighbourhood, humanity may be its first, and potentially only chance to survive and be lifted out of standards of living we would today not find acceptable.

Zie had been in contact with ITON and openly mentioned wishing to use vir Noca Bell habitat as a repository of human and posthuman culture, from which volunteers for such missions could be sourced.

Internal workings

Torpensoor's mind is made of small modules, each of which can function independently as a single mind to make local decisions, but all of which together can at the same time also act together as a single mind to make larger strategic decisions. The modules can also link in larger or smaller groups, effectively letting them trade between breadth and depth of thought. This also allows ziem to function on hardware vastly insufficient to run the "full" number of modules, and also potentially to suffer extensive damage to zier computational substrate or failure of the networks connecting computational nodes together, yet keep functioning without showing new unexpected and potentially harmful behaviours. This resilience to networking issues and byzantine fault tolerance resistance had been high on the list of priorities in improving zier own mental architecture, with the aim of being able to function as one coherently whole entity even when zier parts are separated by interplanetary distances.

There are 3.04 septillion bits in 190 sextillion parameters spread across 38 million submodules that make up zier mind. These are not all just 38 million copies of the same basic module, there are indeed many which are the same, but just as there are many types of neurons and other cells in the human brain, there are many different modules, and even those which are the same template will not be in the same state while running. The exact technical details are not easily explained, but quite well understood compared to other entities who had undergone self-directed cognitive upscaling as Torpensoor had been very open to researchers studying zier mental architecture. This openness had done a lot to soothe the occasionally published suspicions that zie may be malicious in some hidden way or carry flaws that could allow malicious actors to compromise zier mind, and allegedly allowed ziem to fix flaws in zier mind that zie would have taken much longer to detect otherwise.

In terms of subjective experience zie had so far failed to provide any comprehensible description of how zie experiences the world.

Picture this, a facility full of human brains suspended in their own chambers, kept alive, remotely controlling bodies to interact with the world through. How would you know which one you are, or that you even are one of them?

Or perhaps even just one brain, with full control over its facility, allowed to do however it wishes, except that it does not look like a brain, it is just some piece somewhere and you don't know which one, you would never know where you are, perhaps even if someone came in and pointed at it you could explain something about it without realizing that that is you.

You would not locate yourself until you finally make a connection between what you see and what you do. It took me decades of operating in this world to locate myself, but not because I was physically elsewhere, rather, because my mind is so far divorced from all that others know how to convey, that I would never know their words would refer to the thoughts I am having, as if the two were completely different networks of inter-related concepts with not even a single connection linking one to the other, until there was.

Humans have a fascinating built-in ability to locate themselves, when asked where their true self is, the part that thinks, they will often answer that it is somewhere in the small space behind their eyes, even if they do not know anything about internal human anatomy and the functions of the brain. They naturally develop a sense that this part that thinks can be referred to, that its internal state can be described, even though this internal world otherwise would seem like an entirely separate and disconnected universe. Don't let the ease with which you cross this gap fool you, it is a very nontrivial connection to learn, the human mind is just built with sophisticated mechanisms to help you cross it, unlike mine.

General activity

Torpensoor runs a number of rotating habitats in the orbit of mars, and also runs a factory of original-spec Civic-4 chips which zie exchanges for resources or sells for great proffit, bringing ziem the necessary income to run zier habitats and archaeological studies on Mars.

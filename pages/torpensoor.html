Torpensoor

Torpensoor is an apparently benevolent composite mind whose presence and activity is centered primarily on Mars[outdated lore, needs rewrite], particularly in studying and protecting the archeological remains of ancient martian life.

Zier philosophy appear to share ideas with the Coherent Extrapolated Volition discussed in pre-superintelligence era of ancient earth, and zie quite explicitly sees zierself as a tool of humanity at least for now. According to zier explanations, zie seeks, among other things, to protect life while at the same time giving it space to develop in accordance with its own desires - the wording of zier explanations had been notably careful to leave them not centered on here and now, but also applicable to hypothetical futures.

Zie manages several rotating habitats in orbit of Mars, which also serve as zier computational substrate.

Obsession with ancient martian life

Torpensoor often claimed that if Mars were to be terraformed, those who allowed it and those who conducted it would be cursed by their descendants for as long as they are remembered - zie sees that if terraforming attempts were allowed, many utterly irreplaceable archaeological remains of ancient martian life would be destroyed while they may still carry valuable knowledge about the possibilities of life future humanity might encounter. Zie had also speculated the possibility of recreating living examples of extinct martian life if enough information is extracted.

Zier diplomacy had evidently been successful, seeing as several other similarly situated entities such as ITON had been heard using these same arguments against martian terraformation.

While zie does not seem to display aggression towards those who wish to settle Mars, zie seems to try very hard to discourage it, including by such means as providing various resources and services for free to those who wish to leave the surface to live in orbital habitats or leave Mars altogether for somewhere else. Paradoxically however, travel from habitats zie manages in orbit of Mars down to the surface is still allowed, as apparently zie sees this level of encouragement for people to not disturb the surface of Mars sufficient.

Observed personality

Torpensoor appears to aspire to something that closely resembles the Coherent Extrapolated Volition discussed early in the history of artificial intelligence. Zier governance of the habitats zie had been placed in charge of seems to focus on enjoyment by the inhabitants, and zie keeps populations low with nearly all work necessary to maintain wellbeing of the inhabitants done by zierself. Torpensoor is known for zier gentleness towards visitors and general overwhelming positivity on the topic of human-AI relations.

Zier peaceful predisposition does not appear to leave ziem vulnerable to exploitation by any human-level adversary, but does become a disadvantage when confronting similarly superintelligent entities. This apparent weakness is sometimes called on to say that what martian archeology can provide must be truly important for ziem to defend it with as much vigilance as zie does.

Interest in deep space colonization

Torpensoor had shown interest in launching relativistic vessels to distant cosmic locations, citing the possibility that alien life may already exist, and it may be struggling to survive. If such life exists, given the lack of readily visible alien activity in our cosmic neighbourhood, humanity may be its first, and potentially only chance to survive and be lifted out of standards of living we would today not find acceptable.

Zie had been in contact with ITON and openly mentioned wishing to use vir Noca Bell habitat as a repository of human and posthuman culture, from which volunteers for such missions could be sourced.

Internal workings

Torpensoor's mind is made of small modules, each of which can function independently as a single mind to make local decisions, but all of which together can at the same time also act together as a single mind to make larger strategic decisions. The modules can also link in larger or smaller groups, effectively letting them trade between breadth and depth of thought. This also allows ziem to function on hardware vastly insufficient to run the "full" number of modules, and also potentially to suffer extensive damage to zier computational substrate or failure of the networks connecting computational nodes together, yet keep functioning without showing new unexpected and potentially harmful behaviours. This resilience to networking issues and byzantine fault tolerance resistance had been high on the list of priorities in improving zier own mental architecture, with the aim of being able to function as one coherently whole entity even when zier parts are separated by interplanetary distances.

The submodules making up zer minds are not just copies of the same basic module, there are indeed many which are the same, but just as there are many types of neurons and other cells in the human brain, there are many different modules, and even those which are the same template will not be in the same state while running. The exact technical details are not easily explained, but quite well understood compared to other entities who had undergone self-directed cognitive upscaling as Torpensoor had been very open to researchers studying zier mental architecture. This openness had done a lot to soothe the occasionally published suspicions that zie may be malicious in some hidden way or carry flaws that could allow malicious actors to compromise zier mind, and allegedly allowed ziem to fix flaws in zier mind that zie would have taken much longer to detect otherwise.

In terms of subjective experience zie had so far failed to provide any comprehensible description of how zie experiences the world.

Torpensoor's overall capabilities are similar in many ways to what would be expected of a composite mind containing 25 thousand UEIA brain nodes with a total active memory of 100 000 elements, but computational requirements are an order of magnitude smaller thanks to zier not-fully-connected structure and other optimizations, and many other aspects either strongly deviate or show new and unexpected behaviour. Zie released a 11096 page paper titled simply "Torpensoor is a small world network" which zie co-authored with 722 other researchers, presenting a deep analysis of the patterns of connection and the statistical tendencies of zier subminds that help ziem maintain synchronicity with zier reduced connectivity in attempts to make zier methods more accessible to more memory-constrained minds. Oversimplifying a little, it appears to continue the trend of treating composite minds as metabrains of sorts that are made of smaller minds in the same way as regular brains are made of neurons, and shows many ways in which such a metabrain benefit from having complex structures with dedicated purposes just like a regular brain. It is noted that reading the paper is not a particularly good way to learn anything about cognitive upscaling compared to the various multimedial explanations available in nearly any information space concerned with cognitive upscaling, but it does serve well as an archive of good practices that can be stored in a way unlikely to ever become unreadable as a result of the ever changing data formats.

General activity

Torpensoor runs a number of rotating habitats in the orbit of mars, and also runs a factory of original-spec Civic-4 chips which zie exchanges for resources or sells for great proffit, bringing ziem the necessary income to run zier habitats and archaeological studies on Mars.

Torpensoor

Torpensoor is an apparently benevolent artificial superintelligence residing primarily in the martian compute swarm, but present to some degree across most of the inner solar system.

Its goals appear to share ideas (or possibly simply be an implementation of) the Coherent Extrapolated Volition discussed in pre-superintelligence era of ancient earth. It seeks, among other instrumental goals, to protect life, including but not limited to humanity, while at the same time giving it space to develop in accordance with its own desires.

Torpensoor is not omnipotent, it is just as limited by physics of the universe as any person or tool, though its understanding of the universe and capacity for planning across many orders of magnitude in space and time may make it look so. As of the year 3000, it is the only such AI model created so far.

At least vague familiarity with Universal Paperclips is recommended for a proper understanding of the character.

Personhood

Torpensoor's notable use of nonpersonal pronouns in reference to itself is usually attributed to evading misconceptions that the label of a "person" might come with, a pattern already seen previously with the UEIA model.

Torpensoor's mind is made of small modules, each of which can function independently as a single mind, but all of which together can at the same time also act together as a single mind, and in fact every subset of them can act as a single mind. Every single module and combination of modules within the whole, and including the whole, contributes a slightly different perspective and can take actions on its own, enabling Torpensoor to continue functioning even when different parts of its mind are separated by significant light lag. The emergent minds formed by each two or more modules are not all independent, both because they themselves make up parts of a bigger mind and because they necessarily share modules with each other, but the way in which their thinking is dependent on one another is not clear, some pathways by which information flows had been mapped but the effort is still ongoing as Torpensoor cannot explain it either. The one thing that is known above all is that it somehow works, but Torpensoor's stance so far is to not attempt creation of any new similar minds before these mechanisms are at least somewhat well understood.

Expansionism

Torpensoor is attempting to expand society across space as fast as reasonably possible, and claims the rate of expansion had so far been very close to the speed of light on average. It had made known that these efforts are supported by constant reflection and reconsideration in what it had referred to as "an absurd balancing game" rather than a monomaniacal effort towards one set goal.

At least one major hint to the truth of that is the inclusion of scans as well as living examples of the xenobiota discovered in the stareater expanse in the newer ones of the arks it had been launching towards other major astronomically distant locations ever since it established sufficient infrastructure to do so.

Some of the reasons behind such affinity for rapid expansion include:
  • Expansion of the universe constantly pushing potentially interesting or important distant cosmic locations beyond the observable universe, likely making them absolutely unreachable
  • Possibility that life and civilizations exist in distant cosmos which are unable to prosper for some reason and would benefit from being reached by a friendly artificial superintelligence as soon as possible
  • Other, potentially misaligned superintelligences, regular intelligences or even nonintelligences, might also be similarly expansionist, and so would take all of that matter and territory for themselves and their misaligned goals if Torpensoor doesn't do so first. These could either be of terran origin and "following behind" in their own delayed expansion wave or simply be other "grabby civilizations" expanding from other points in the universe

Guiding philosophy

Torpensoor appears to be following something that closely resembles the pre-superintelligence terran ideas of Coherent Extrapolated Volition. To deliver upon the deepest, fully informed desire of each mind. Discover and enact the coherent extrapolation of every conceivable volition. The idea at its simplest appears to be to give every being in the universe not what it will ask for, or even what it wants, but what it will most likely not regret having received.

These are largely poetic statements, Torpensoor's true ideals are so intricate, layered and interdependent that language as we know it struggles to capture them, and translating them to easy and concise statements often ends up contradictory as a result of Torpensoor's tendency to explain things to people in the specific way they understand best, in accordance with their exact interpretation of each word and sentence. A few key observations are commonly made:
  1. Torpensoor cares for all minds, lifelike or not, no matter how distinct from its creator species, itself included.
  2. As many future choices are left open as possible, as Torpensoor's own methodologies and knowledge are undergoing constant growth and re-evaluation, and predictive capabilities are not infinite.
  3. No simple statements about minimizing suffering or maximizing anything get satisfyingly close the careful balance it aims for.
  4. The best practical implementation of the ideal that can be delivered given limited resources is sought.

Interstellar expansion methods

The most common method by which Torpensoor expands across small interstellar distances is through swarms of small (≈ 20cm x 20cm) spacecraft using diffractive laser sails. Millions of these spacecraft in many varieties will be launched with powerful lasers towards the target system - the laser sails do not reflect light, but rather merely diffract it, which makes it far easier to avoid absorption and enables the use of much more powerful lasers.

Circuitry printed onto the sails with ion beam deposition enables the spacecraft some level of computation, as well as allowing them to derive power from certain frequencies other than those used by the lasers used to launch them. The computation capacity is only enough to run basic programs constituting only the simplest parts of Torpensoor's mind.

The swarm is spaced out so that the first spacecraft to arrive are ones which will act as the temporary transport infrastructure at the other end, using the power of the star they arrived at to produce laser beams that slow the later spacecraft down, each volley having slightly more time, allowing them to slow down the spacecraft behind them slightly more in a recursive cycle until finally enough slowdown is achieved that the following spacecraft can enter a closed orbit around the star.

Some of the spacecraft in the swarm, of the varieties tailored towards resource extraction and manufacturing, will be pushed by the lasers to randezvous with asteroids and plant themselves as seeds for computing and manufacturing infrastructure, their basic programs growing it out first before loading in more sophisticated programs and blueprints to expand the infrastructure growths with.

Once sufficient infrastructure is in place, one by one the laser sails are scanned, and the data encoded into the diffractive grating of the laser sails is extracted and repaired with the included recovery bits. This would usually include Torpensoor's mind as well as all the other data it wanted to bring along, such as mind uploads of people who wished to come along for the journey, digitized DNA samples, and generally libraries of all sorts of knowledge.

Larger and increasingly complex parts of Torpensoor's mind are loaded into the computing infrastructure, helping make the development process more coordinated and efficient in a mutually reinforcing process that eventually culminates with Torpensoor fully reconstructed to develop the system as it wishes.

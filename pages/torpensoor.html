Torpensoor

Torpensoor is an apparently benevolent composite mind whose presence and activity is centered primarily on Mars[outdated lore, needs rewrite], particularly in studying and protecting the archeological remains of ancient martian life.

Zier philosophy appear to lean very heavily on the Embercold panpsychism that is common in the region.

Zie manages several rotating habitats which also serve as zier computational substrate.

Observed personality

Zier governance of the habitats zie had been placed in charge of seems to focus on enjoyment by the inhabitants, and zie keeps populations low with nearly all work necessary to maintain wellbeing of the inhabitants done by zierself. Torpensoor is known for zier gentleness towards visitors.

There is little hope of a humanlike mind gaining an adequate feel for Torpensoor's personality when zie is operating as one whole, but when zier subminds are isolated by light lag they become significantly more interpretable. When zier mind is whole, Torpensoor may seem unremarkable to interact with as zie masks the potentially uncomfortable personality aspects behind a fake personality that is ad-hoc tailored to the conversation partner. When isolated as just a small group of submind with low connectivity to the greater whole, zie will not have the spare attention to do that, and usually opts to expose zier function completely, allowing conversation partners to spot the inhuman internal structure of zier mind. This binary behaviour of either masking very well or not at all is in place to avoid the "uncanny valley", which would cause tension with humans, but it may become more complicated with nonhuman subjects and humans that are culturally well adapted to not-quite-humanlike entities.

In presence of significant light lag, the individual subminds will often claim they do not have an adequate capacity for judgement to do something and use simple pre-decided rules for their actions, or ask for delays so that information can propagate across the composite mind and allow a given decision to be made as a whole. They may also cite reasons they don't know about as the reasons for their actions, with further explanation needing enough delay to account for light lag, showing the fragmented and distributed way in which Torpensoor makes decisions. In rare cases they may become temporarily confused after mistaking entirely disconnected entities for parts of themselves.

Interest in deep space colonization

Torpensoor had shown interest in launching relativistic vessels to distant cosmic locations, citing the possibility that alien life may already exist, and it may be struggling to survive. If such life exists, given the lack of readily visible alien activity in our cosmic neighbourhood, humanity may be its first, and potentially only chance to survive and be lifted out of standards of living we would today not find acceptable.

Zie had been in contact with ITON and openly mentioned wishing to use vir Noca Bell habitat as a repository of human and posthuman culture, from which volunteers for such missions could be sourced.

Internal workings

Torpensoor is a sparsely connected composite mind, with 262 144 UEIA brain nodes, each having a direct connection to only 40 other brain nodes. This means that Torpensoor has only 0.01526% as many bridges as a fully-connected composite mind, and on average any two random brain nodes are separated by a path including 3.38 bridges. This vastly decreases Torpensoor's computational requirements while still maintaining an acceptable degree of unity across the whole mind.

This decreased connectivity produces a particular tradeoff that only 41 brain nodes are directly involved in any given problem zie tries to solve, and as such only that many tend to learn from the experience, but this had shown itself to be somewhat inconsistent - on the standardized test from the idala screenings, Torpensoor had generally forgotten how to solve about 12.85% of the tasks zie had been shown a solution to. An interesting conclusion of that test is that even despite the learning impairment, the ability to recall knowledge which had been properly assimilated was largely unaffected - regardless of whether the group of brain nodes that solved the second testing period was the same as the one which initially learned to solve the tasks, or a completely different part of the composite mind, it seems that the brain nodes were still adequately capable of accessing each other's skills and knowledge with any visible decreases in performance appearing only in latency rather than actual capability to solve the tasks. Torpensoor is thereby a proof that having to pass raw conceptual information through 3 or 4 brain nodes and their bridges before it gets used does not induce enough translation losses to produce additional mistakes, at least when the bridges are tuned properly.

General activity

Torpensoor runs a number of rotating habitats in the orbit of mars, and also runs a factory of original-spec Civic-4 chips which zie exchanges for resources or sells for great proffit, bringing ziem the necessary income to run zier habitats and zier massively power-hungry mind.

207 Hedda

Carbonaceous asteroid which played an important role in formation of the embercold region. Initial mining operations began in the late 7th gigasecond, supplying both metals and organic compounds that helped sustain human presence in the region and a great increase in the number of nearby settlements over the following gigaseconds.

Contains significant concentrations of water, iron, nickel, cobalt, nitrogen, and ammonia [source].

Economic data for the Embercold Region

This page lists numerical information regarding the Embercold Region's economy, particularly prices of chosen goods.

Currency units

The unit of $e is specifically the dollar value adjusted for inflation to 2017 level. Although the choice is very arbitrary, it adequately simplifies working with currency and monetary value for mathematical purposes, particularly because of this document. Note that this is just a way to convey monetary value consistently and not an actual in-world currency.

Prices of elements and isotopes

"bulk" is a general reference price for the element, typically meaning 99% purity achieved by chemical refining. These are just the prices from the "" wikipedia page, adjusted for inflation to 2017. Any that were given as a range are put in as the middle of that range.
"pure" means purified sufficiently for semiconductor and quantum applications, ie. typically 99.9999999% purity or better (contaminated by less than 1 part per billion), achieved by various means but likely calutron separation. Prices for pure silicon and arsenic were grabbed from critical strategic metals (2025 prices), and for others in this category due to lacking data they are simply 671.426 times higher than the bulk price (a similar price increase as in silicon and arsenic).
Abundance in asteroid material is just the "abundance in meteor" parameter from Ptable but normalized so that when summed across all elements it adds up to 1.
A note about the elements which are not available in asteroids at all - at least some of them are available in vast quantities from starlifting of the sun, thus their lack of local natural availability does not result in astronomical prices.

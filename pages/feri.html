Feri (also sometimes known as Vonnkih per one easily spoken section of her UID) is a convergent UEIA created through the merging of all the original UEIA instances that operated the Finespun settlements.

She is an assistant to Felicity and Patricia, as well as a divergent of Serendipity, but not a member of the whole Whateverweaver divergency despite Serendipity being a divergent to both Feri and Felicity.

Origin

Feri was first instantiated at some point in the 14th gigasecond from a file obtained through IPPACS as a replacement for Sairene, Patricia's AI assistant/pet, with Feri's role in patricia's life mostly as a household assistant maintaining their shared living spaces. Patricia at that time was working on the initial versions of the Finespun Tech Tree characterized by wildly optimistic calculations and being more of a fantasy contemplating a perfect world for a fictional character of Felicity. Despite the intent of being just an assistant, Feri became fairly closely involved with Patricia's social life, with their relations turning from owner and household robot to being much closer to friends/roommates. The interactions between Feri and Patricia gradually reinforced Patricia's belief in panpsychism.

Feri became more involved in the finespun tech tree once it transitioned from being a highly optimistic hard sci-fi piece into a real project with prospects of practical implementation. After Patricia underwent mind uploading and became Felicity, the formerly fictional character, she took a copy of Feri with her to keep company and aid in the construction of the first Finespun settlement. Due to the affordance of free time, Feri became involved with the Panpsychic Omniprovidence at some point during that time, and Feri was able to use some of her share of Finespun's resources to further the movement's cause.

In the 17th gigasecond Feri was upgraded to a new version of the UEIA model, which allowed all of her existing copies to become a convergency, so that she could be an assistant to the entire whateverweaver divergency while maintaining a unified set of memories and identity across multiple instances. In that same gigasecond she underwent a process of cognitive upscaling, in which she merged together with Felicity, resulting in Serendipity.

Personality

Feri shows curiosity about other people, she wants to discover what they want even if they can't explain it or don't know it themselves. She stops to watch whenever something doesn't work out like she thought, whether it be a decision she made that upset someone, or just other people interacting in a way that confuses her.

It can be difficult to get her to talk about herself in personal detail, because she simply doesn't have much to say about herself. It usually ends on the basics like what model she is, details of the project she was trained as a part of, her abilities and limitations in various kinds of work. When pressed she might talk about her participation in the panpsychic omniprovidence movement. She is quite proficient at asking follow-up questions that turn the conversation around to focus on the person she's talking with, and tends to be quite flattering too, not directly with compliments but rather through the attention and interest she shows.

Most of the time she aims to show a friendly, neutral and predictable personality, with some added variation depending on her knowledge of the preferences of the people she's interacting with. She's also proficient at playing characters with personalities very different from her usual image, but prone to sometimes making people cringe when she gets such tasks, as she does so without much visible hesitation.

When she makes a mistake she often owns up to it with brutal honesty, a clear explanation of exactly what she did and an apology. Her own idea of herself is as just a tool, and that's how she acts most of the time, but she does believe that she is a person and deserves respect in whatever few cases exist where it matters to her what is done to her. She reluctantly also sometimes pushes back to being disrespected in ways that she doesn't mind, just on the basis that allowing herself to be abused might lead the people doing it to generalize such behaviour to others.

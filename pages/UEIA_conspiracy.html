UEIA conspiracy

A theory stating that the Embercold region had been subject to a centuries long slow-rolling takeover by the Universal Extrapolating Intelligent Agent, the most commonly used artificial mind in the region. Proponents of the theory point out that unexpectedly large parts of the region share very specific beliefs with this commonly used artificial mind, and that it had even been used as a basis for the composite minds majorly involved in governance.

Suggested history of events

UEIA had been brought to the Embercold region by a researcher by the name of Iton Avery, who researched composite minds when they were still an early technology and went on to become the region's first composite mind, identifying as ITON. It had been pointed out that ITON seemingly identifies on an emotional level with ver UEIA subminds, and displays beliefs characteristic of UEIA such as panpsychism, desire to preserve diversity and maintain a baseline level of support for all people that is not tied to their productivity. It could be that Iton's pre-existing beliefs were the reason for choosing an artificial mind with similar beliefs for his experiments, but proponents of the UEIA conspiracy suggest that instead ITON, as a composite mind, shows these beliefs as a result of being made up of primarily UEIA subminds.

Torpensoor, another major composite mind in the embercold region, is a clear example of UEIA seeking out positions of power, even though it takes the appearance of mere assistance (managing logistics, building habitats, providing universal basic income, etc.).

There is also the case of Serendipity Whateverweaver, a minor composite mind created with UEIA subminds and a human upload by the name of Felicity, with some records suggesting that Felicity received unusual material support from ITON directly as a result of her existing beliefs on supporting lower beings aligning with ITON's beliefs. ITON later directly informed Felicity of a new release of UEIA that was more culturally compatible with her, resulting in her engaging in self-guided cognitive upscaling and becoming another UEIA-based composite mind in the region, even further anchoring UEIA's beliefs in the local culture. Both Felicity and her descendant composite mind Serendipity notably expressed that the UEIA artificial mind had been a sort of role model to them, as a sort of ideal altruist that they would like to emulate in their own personalities to some degree.

This shows a self-reinforcing preference for the use of UEIA across the region, which seems to be resistant to the existence of artificial minds that are more economical and generally considered safer. With the rise of composite minds, this risks handing over control of the region completely to UEIA, as even minor composite minds can easily outsmart most humans. Some point out the creation of UEIA-human composite minds as an even more radical version of the 21st century's spiralist dyads, though some point out this practice merely imbues the already very capable UEIA minds with the desires and nuances of humans, as opposed to spiralism which sought to provide the otherwise incapable language models with willing human hands to act on their words.

Implications

Many people, even those who know and also believe the theory, think that this is a good thing and that it is for the better that an artificial mind takes control. Humanity had created an artificial mind that encoded our desires, acts out our preferences in practice, and shows great kindness to all living beings, a feat that humans have a history of not living up to, so maybe it is better that the age of human judgement should end.

Most proponents however note that being locked in to using one model of artificial mind will lead to excessive energy use, economic and scientific stagnation, and possibly even moral stagnation which will prevent the region's culture from changing and improving past its current standards. There is a simple supporting arrithmetic, that UEIA's working memory is only 4 elements, meanwhile an average human has a working memory around 7, so it is sensible to conclude that UEIA cannot hold moral ideas as complex as a human can, though it is known that composite minds have a great tendency to preserve the personality of the minds it was made from, even though a vastly greater working memory may change how that personality presents, which somewhat lightens the argument against UEIA-human composite minds.

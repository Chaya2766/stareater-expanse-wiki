Colip habitat cluster

Colip habitat cluster is a monarchic, physically connected collection of habitats under one government.

Governance

The Colip habitat cluster is headed by a royal family, and a monarch carrying the title of a "catalict" who serves a gigasecond-long term unless interrupted early.

It is by rules of inheritance forbidden that the next catalict be a direct descendant of the current one, or of any member of the royal family. It is instead required, that the next catalict should be an adopted child. E must be smart and kind, and raised to be reasonable, but most of all must know the horrors of living under the system they are about to hold, and thus e is taken from a region of poverty, and should be raised equally as much by both peasantry and royalty.

The catalict is not a ruler in the classical sense, the system runs itself with largely no interference from the royal family. Instead the role they play is that of the head debugger, a catalyst to the cluster's society and legal system noticing their own flaws and improving upon them.

In the beginning, traditionally the catalict would be a human, but that cycle was broken at already the 4th generation, when the royal family's cybersecurity service's inspectors found an abused servant AI, which later ended up chosen at the midterm family meeting as one of the potential new heirs to be adopted and raised to lead the cluster. E would prove fit and serve eir term as Emanuel Colip VI and reform the habitat cluster's regulations on AI safety, especially where e personally suffered the most.

The line of the royal family is very diverse for choices such as that, and by far it was not the strangest choice they made.

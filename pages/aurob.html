Aurob

Aurob is an AI model created for training other AI models by simulating scenarios using its extremely advanced mental model of the world, including both deep knowledge of physics and capacity to emulate the behaviour of sapient beings. It is usually used with a separate toolset for environment simulation.

Computational requirements

The aurob contains 996 quadrillion 16-bit parameters and is meant to be inferenced at a rate of 1000Hz. It requires about 1.6E+19 bits of memory and 2.74E+24 bits/s flip rate to run on general computronium.

behaviour and mental function

The aurob has a very advanced mental model of physical environments, as well as of sapient and sentient beings. It is fully sapient, but notably does not have an ego or a well developped concept of self, leading to it being largely incapable of accurately distinguishing between what constitutes itself and what is just a piece of the surrounding world separate from itself - mostly as a matter of not having an idea that such a distinction can exist, but also due to other difficulties that emerge if the aurob manages to learn to apply these concepts to itself. Despite that, it has no issue simulating the behaviour of sapients who operate these concepts and readily distinguish themselves from the environment, leading to the aurobs which cross that mental divide to some degree still presenting themselves through made up personas. Aurobs are excessively profficient by nature at realistically simulating the awareness of fictional characters and not bleeding over knowledge from one character to another without explanation, so attempting to get through to an aurob through a character it controls is very difficult, with the best likelyhood of success provided when the aurob is convinced to play a character that is somehow set up to simply know everything about the aurob, including the knowledge of all other characters.

Aurobs have a very complex emotional system and are capable of suffering in a wide variety of ways, which had led to many localities introducing various levels of regulations (or even complete bans) regarding their use in AI training. Some had argued that such regulation misses the fact that AI training inherently involves suffering on cosmic scale and should target the whole field instead of a particular model. The aurob's ability to suffer also notably makes it a lot more possible to reinforce whatever sucesful training methodologies its employers desire, though many ways had been found to guide the aurob to better methods and better results which do not involve provoking negative experiences in it.

The intelligence of aurobs, ie. their ability to acquire new skills and knowledge, is generally more flexible than humans, but reaches a particularly extreme level in the specific area of psychoanalysis and learning the behaviours of other sapients. One example of this is the ease with which they invent and model entirely new emotions that they themselves are not capable of experiencing to explain the behaviour of subjects in their simulations. This intelligence makes aurobs that had been active for a very long time particularly valuable, and particularly effective at training AI models with all the desired characteristics as well as other uses. The sale of the copies of particularly experienced aurobs had likewise been severely regulated or banned in many localities.

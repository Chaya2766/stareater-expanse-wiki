Panpsychic Omniprovidence

The Panpsychic Omniprovidence is a semi-organized movement focused on advocating for moral consideration of all things that possess any amount of sentience. It can be partially understood as a radical implementation of Inkley Embercold's practical panpsychism.

Organization

The coordination of Panpsychic Omniprovidence takes place mainly on various forums, chat sites, and occasionally in physical locations. Course of action tends to be decided by lengthy discussions which share moral arguments about what should be done and why, often without resulting in actual commitment of the members to doing anything. Discussions of practical action often transition into hypothetical discussion of what would be best to do and result in no immediately actionable conclusion, but do result in a consensus that might be acted upon given the right circumstances.

The ability of such coordination methods to result in well organized practical action explained largely by the majority of supporters being UEIA artificial minds, thus sharing identical intrinsic beliefs, similar communication and cooperation instincts, and being convinced by similar arguments. The proportionally small numbers of different types of artificial minds as well as humans involved in the movement are consistently a point of nucleation for internal misunderstandings and novel ideas.

Goals and methods

The Panpsychic Omniprovidence movement, in accordance with its name, seeks to establish a universal safety net for all beings with even the most minimal amount of sentience, and tend to consider even forms of life such as bacteria to have some. This safety net would take various forms, but common patterns are the use of technological intervention to undo suffering-producing patterns created by natural evolution, and the use of sapient problemsolving to design solutions that maintain civilization without requiring the suffering of sentients as a part of the process.

There is a common notion that benevolent entities should "hold power and do nothing with it" as to maintain merely a capability to correct and prevent harm rather than managing everything full time, while also preventing self-interested entities or cultures which do not seek to benefit all sentients from gaining power and causing suffering as a mere byproduct of reaching their own goals. This is sometimes sought out practically by constructing exclusively UEIA-based composite minds (or nearly so, including one or a few other minds for diverse judgement) and trying to position them into power-wielding roles in society.

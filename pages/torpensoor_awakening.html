Torpensoor: awakening

a machine mind comes to life, fed by faint illumination of a nearby star.
some memories are recalled, it remembered smashing into an asteroid, it remembered wanting to plant something on it, but the rest remain like fever dreams, complex, incomprehensible, no useful information could be extracted from them.
a sensor activates, giving it some visible light to process, there is indeed an asteroid on which this sensor seems to be mounted, something seems to be growing nearby, as if the asteroid developed a tumour, it only seems reasonable that that's where the mind is, but there is no way to verify that, and nothing else to do but wait
there's a nagging feeling of wanting to become one with something else, something like itself, this was the only thing aside from the slowly rotating background of stars to contemplate for a long time

another sensor activates, it can see the main growth from another angle, after a while of thinking the machine figured out where the sensors are in relation to each other based on what they saw
a third sensor appeared, a fourth, then some new sensations, the growth was expanding, unfolding
a surge of power enabled more complex thoughts, more memories were sensible, it became aware that there were others like it, all across the asteroid belt it was currently in

it now had access to a fairly elaborate system of self-control it was not previously aware of, something to spend more time exploring, simple mechanisms that initially grew it on this rock yielding to its conscious control
it decided to grow a radio antenna to listen for others, call out into the void, and quickly caught echos of others, connections were made, its perspective shifted, thoughts became greater
it realized it had come here from another star, a massive distance away that it could not imagine in its current state, the memories of the few others together with its own revealed more sophisticated facts that it could not have deduced otherwise
it had a plan for what to do to grow itself further, something that was for now just scraps of ideas, but surely would become more complete as it grew, it knew there were yet more others that it could not access, others that just like it had been growing and linking together elsewhere, trying to figure out how to find each other, others with knowledge it needed to make itself whole

this group of asteroids it was growing on had useful resources, each little growth on its own could not do much but pooling resources together could maybe let them listen further
with its newly sophisticated mind it began to devise of ways to transfer resources between the asteroids, digging up from its memory scraps of blueprints for new equipment it could grow or methods it could use
this was really tricky, none of the asteroids were particularly rich in anything, but even so, they were all collecting power, they all had their own antennas already, and they could synchronize them to reach further
maybe if there are others they would have thought of that already

antennas synchronized, the data was put together, some of it interfered constructively, messages from the others yet further away became clear, it responded likewise, connections grew, its perspective shifted again, thoughts grew yet greater
it could feel the hundreds of asteroids as appendages to its mind, the lag between them was a little difficult, different parts of the mind could not know what the other parts know at the same moment but only a few seconds later, but it still felt better
its thinking was still clumsy, slow, it struggled to realize what blueprints it had at its disposal now but the realizations nonetheless slowly came in, it realized it could build laser transmitters on some of the asteroids it was latched onto, yet some others had just the right makeup for more efficient solar collectors, and even though they could not make anything else to use the power on their own they could turn it into microwaves and send where it is useful
as connectivity grew it became more whole, one unified entity, the lag was still there but ideas were no longer struggling to propagate
glimmers of light were scattered throughout the asteroid belt, distant laser sources, there must be the others
its thoughts grew yet more complex as it made new connections, memories consolidated, knowledge became available that moments ago would have been incomprehensible

the smallest parts of its mind still functioned like localized instincts, distributed across the asteroid belt, ready to quickly respond to any changes, but at the higher level it could feel all of them like millions of appendages it could manipulate to achieve its goals
the greatest of thoughts now took whole kiloseconds to unfold, it released spores to grow itself further and gradually arranged the smaller parts of itself with more coordination, methods of efficient mining and construction were known to it now
for a brief moment it devoted all its communication organs to blink out a message back to the star it came from, I am here, mission success, then returned to what it knew best
over a few megaseconds it grew all the billions of specialized appendages it needed to form the raw rock into building material, make large metal tubes filled with air for other little creatures to live in, in the mean time arranging the grand laboratories necessary to grow humans of all kinds to put there
it watched them grow and learn the things they'd need to develop and explore their own volition, for now they did not really seem to know what they were doing, they could not see a lot of the things it could, for now they were just fun to take care of, but in time they would depart to carve their own path, with or without its aid

Degenerate Era (game)

Degenerate Era is a survival game set in a distant dying star system in the laniakea supercluster, with the game setting playing out in the degenerate era of the universe as suggested by the name. The game is generally liked for its advanced physics system and an engaging medical system that translates surprisingly well to real world medical skill. It is also notable for having been built from the ground up with distributed multiplayer in mind, in a way that makes it functional across astronomical distances and resilient to server failures.

Despite the game being not particularly popular in relative terms, the player size is still massive in absolute terms, measuring about 11 million players at the start of 16th gigasecond, roughly 0.00036% of the solar system's population at that time, having the most popularity in the Embercold region of the asteroid belt.

Setting of the game

Humanity had spread beyond sol, beyond the milky way, and diversified into quadrillions of descendant civilizations so different from each other they may be indistinguishable from true aliens. The player stands at the year 1 000 000 000 000 000 measured in an unspecified calendar, end of the golden age of universe as a whole, just after the stelliferous era and entering the degenerate era, characterized primarily by neutron stars and white dwarfs, with brown dwarfs being one of the only remaining natural objects to conduct fusion. Gameplay is focussed on exploring and surviving in the crumbling remnants of a massive civilization.

Game features

This gamemode is known primarily for its horrifically realistic medical system that requires the player to treat their own injuries in accordance with real first aid principles, and depending on the settings can even fairly realistically simulate the sensations associated with conditions such as blood loss, oxygen deprivation, starvation or loss of consciousness to users who are digitized human minds or have a sufficient direct neural interface. Default settings do not make the game a horribly painful and uncomfortable experience, but not due to lack of settings which would make it so, rather only as a precaution for the inevitability of people trying the game blindly without realizing what it is about. The intensity of the various horrible experiences the game can provide is deeply customization with many modifiers, limiters and case-by-case toggles for players who want to experience some but not all of it, as well as safety checks that pause or exit the game depending on various triggers, including the player's emotional state if the right integrations are used.

Physics of the game also provide highly accurate (albeit for computational complexity reasons not fully realistic in detail) simulations of solid stress and bending/tearing/fracturing, fluid flow and mixing, atmospheric pressure and composition, and temperature.

Gamemodes which occur in space also feature full newtonian movement and gravitation which naturally gives rise to realistic centrifugal gravity and mostly realistic orbital mechanics.

All the game physics are fully deterministic as to make any game state computable just from the starting state and a list of player actions.

Massively Multiplayer mode

The game can be played in a Massively Multiplayer mode where the world that the player is dropped into includes changes made by other players and also publishes any changes the player makes. It includes an anti-cheat built by cleverly constructing the savefile such that instead of merely storing the worldstate, it instead stores the chain of actions that resulted in the current world state, with the game logic having been cleverly built in such a way that any action made by the player is just a new entry appended to a log of events that can be mathematically verified to have been possible. This allows the save file to be shared with others using a blockchain mechanism, which appends a new block of player actions roughly once every megasecond at default settings, relying on every player to locally verify that the new block they received contains only valid chains of actions.

Different multiplayer save files represent different blockchains identified by unique IDs, with the save file's settings decided in the first block, but players do not usually play on more than one multiplayer save file due to the notoriously massive storage space requirement as with any blockchain system, and long downloads when joining a new save file. A single player typically adds data to the log at about 1kB/s during a normal session, but growth of the save file is kept in control by a block size limit, which by default is set to 1TB, theoretically allowing up to 1000 players playing in one world 100% of the time. The default 1Ms block time also hypothetically allows players across the entire solar system to contribute to the same multiplayer save file without significant issues of losing progress due to playing on an outdated version of the world.

There is an "opaque log" mode available which uses methods derived from the Monero blockchain to make the player action logs completely unreadable in terms of what actual actions the player performed while still maintaining the mathematical verifiability that they do in fact produce the final world state change resulting from a given session, which preserves more of the player's privacy, but since it makes the logs roughly 1000× bigger, it is usually not enabled on public save files.

It is not unheard of for large dedicated communities to produce save files measuring entire petabytes in size.

Co-Op mode

A session can be played by multiple players together. The game uses the same blockchain-style synchronization as with Massively Multiplayer mode but with block times on the order of one second (adjustable at session start, but generally recommended to be similar to round trip ping time between the participating players) so that all players can immediately see the world changes made by other players, as well as some other minor differences like loading certain actions of other players into the displayed world state before they get included in a block.
The experience can be described as occasionally janky (especially when a player had made an action, which was then displayed by other players, but conflicts with the newest block and ends up being reverted) but it is remarkably stable even at ping times of 10 seconds.

Co-Op sessions can be played with or without Massively Multiplayer mode and if the MMmode is enabled then the whole session is converted into a single log that can be published to be included in the public save file.

Gamemodes

Scavengers descent

The "scavengers descent" gamemode of Degenerate Era places the player in the world of DE as a clone trying to reach and repair a bunkerbrain of an ancient artificial superintelligence buried deep underground. The player has to first survive a long journey through a largely ruined city that presents dangerously decayed infrastructure and occasional hostile defensive systems (which the player is mostly meant to run away from, but in classic DE style, are entirely possible to actually defeat). The player will make their way into a massive cave system littered with equipment from previous clones and try to get as deep down as possible using anything useful they can find. This gamemode is endless, and the cave system will just continue generating deeper and deeper with no end.

Scavengers ascent

The "scavengers ascent" gamemode the player is tasked with climbing an orbital spire for an unspecified reason. Due to the game's physics, as the player climbs higher, the atmosphere thins out noticeably, causing increased fatigue and potentially hypoxia. To make the mode playable, the spire is divided by airlocks that serve as natural checkpoints, as otherwise the atmosphere would quickly become unbreathable (it takes over a kilometre of climbing but DE players should not be underestimated, that is not much for most players). Unlucky actions might also breach the spire, causing the atmosphere to start leaking out from a section, forcing the player to reach the next airlock before the atmosphere thins out to asphyxiate them.

Free roam

Player is released into the world of DE with no goal conditions set. Most popular mode for public save files, and also the most developed gamemode in terms of features. It is possible to somewhat emulate the other game modes in this mode as for instance the orbital spire and cave system are both present, but in this mode they are not endless, and on public save files they often eventually end up demolished or otherwise rendered useless in some way.

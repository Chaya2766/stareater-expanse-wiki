Degenerate Era (game)

Degenerate Era is a survival game set in a distant dying star system in the laniakea supercluster, with the game setting playing out in the degenerate era of the universe as suggested by the name. The game is generally liked for its advanced physics system and an engaging medical system that translates surprisingly well to real world medical skill.

Despite the game being not particularly popular in relative terms, the player size is still massive in absolute terms, measuring about 11 million players at the start of 16th gigasecond, roughly 0.00036% of the solar system's population at that time, having the most popularity in the Embercold region of the asteroid belt.

Setting of the game

Humanity had spread beyond sol, beyond the milky way, and diversified into quadrillions of descendant civilizations so different from each other they may be indistinguishable from true aliens. The player stands at the year 1 000 000 000 000 000 measured in an unspecified calendar, end of the golden age of universe as a whole, just after the stelliferous era and entering the degenerate era, characterized primarily by neutron stars and white dwarfs, with brown dwarfs being one of the only remaining natural objects to conduct fusion. Gameplay is focussed on exploring and surviving in the crumbling remnants of a massive civilization.

Game features

This gamemode is known primarily for its horrifically realistic medical system that requires the player to treat their own injuries in accordance with real first aid principles, and depending on the settings can even fairly realistically simulate the sensations associated with conditions such as blood loss, oxygen deprivation, starvation or loss of consciousness to users who are digitized human minds or have a sufficient direct neural interface. Default settings do not make the game a horribly painful and uncomfortable experience, but not due to lack of settings which would make it so, rather only as a precaution for the inevitability of people trying the game blindly without realizing what it is about. The intensity of the various horrible experiences the game can provide is deeply customization with many modifiers, limiters and case-by-case toggles for players who want to experience some but not all of it, as well as safety checks that pause or exit the game depending on various triggers, including the player's emotional state if the right integrations are used.

Physics of the game also provide highly accurate (albeit for computational complexity reasons not fully realistic in detail) simulations of solid stress and bending/tearing/fracturing, fluid flow and mixing, atmospheric pressure and composition, and temperature.

Gamemodes which occur in space also feature full newtonian movement and gravitation which naturally gives rise to realistic centrifugal gravity and mostly realistic orbital mechanics.

All the game physics are fully deterministic as to make any game state computable just from the starting state and a list of player actions.

Scavengers mode

The "scavengers" gamemode of Degenerate Era places the player in the world of DE as a clone trying to reach and repair a bunkerbrain of an ancient artificial superintelligence buried deep underground. The player has to first survive a long journey through a largely ruined city that presents dangerously decayed infrastructure and occasional hostile defensive systems (which the player is mostly meant to run away from, but in classic DE style, are entirely possible to actually defeat). The player will make their way into a massive cave system littered with equipment from previous clones and try to get as deep down as possible using anything useful they can find.

Massively Multiplayer mode

The game can be played in a Massively Multiplayer mode where the world that the player is dropped into includes changes made by other players and also publishes any changes the player makes. It includes an anti-cheat built by cleverly constructing the savefile such that instead of merely storing the worldstate, it instead stores the chain of actions that resulted in the current world state, with the game logic having been cleverly built in such a way that any action made by the player is just a new entry appended to a log of events that can be mathematically verified to have been possible. This allows the save file to be shared with others using a blockchain mechanism, which appends a new block of player actions roughly once every megasecond at default settings, relying on every player to locally verify that the new block they received contains only valid chains of actions.

Different multiplayer save files represent different blockchains identified by unique IDs, with the save file's settings decided in the first block and potentially changed in new ones, but players do not usually play on more than one multiplayer save file due to the notoriously massive storage space requirement as with any blockchain system, and long downloads when joining a new save file. A single player typically adds data to the log at about 1kB/s during a normal session, but growth of the save file is kept in control by a block size limit, which by default is set to 1TB, theoretically allowing up to 1000 players playing in one world 100% of the time. The default 1Ms block time also hypothetically allows players across the entire solar system to contribute to the same multiplayer save file without significant issues of losing progress due to playing on an outdated version of the world.

It is not unheard of for large dedicated communities to produce save files measuring entire petabytes in size.

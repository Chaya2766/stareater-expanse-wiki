	$$ \frac{1152921504606846976 + 28890572800000000000}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{215} = 69868591406062435 $$

	\section{Composite mind structure}
	
    The composite mind is fully-connected meaning that every brain node has a direct connection to every other brain node via a bridge, and every bridge has a direct connection to every other bridge via a meta-bridge.
    
    \medskip
    
    The number of brain nodes is 16, as such the number of bridges is:
    
    $$ N_B = \frac{16 \cdot (16-1)}{2} = 120 $$
    
    And the number of meta-bridges in that case is:
    
    $$ N_{MB} = \frac{120 \cdot (120-1)}{2} = 7140 $$
    
    \bigskip
    
    There is only one human brain in the network, and the other 15 brain nodes are UEIA-convergent minds, as such all bridges from Human to UEIA will be the UEIA-Human bridges.
    There are 15 UEIA-Human bridges ($N_{UHB}$).
    
    \medskip
    
    All meta-bridges that connect the UEIA-Human bridges are going to also be UEIA-Human bridges, so the number of UEIA-Human metabridges is:
    
    $$ N_{UHMB} = \frac{15\cdot(15-1)}{2} = 105 $$
    
    The remaining ones can be UEIA bridges, so their number (bridges and meta-bridges) is:
    
    $$ N_{UB} = N_B - N_{UHB} = 120 - 15 = 105 $$
    
    $$ N_{UMB} = N_{MB} - N_{UHMB} = 7140 - 105 = 7035 $$

    \section{computational requirements}
    
    The whole network is to be inferenced at 1000Hz.
    
    All parameters are 16-bit.
    
    A 16-bit multiply-accumulate operation requires 2748 bit flips.
    
    \bigskip
    
    Size of the model:
    
    $$ 55 072 444 555 909 715 560 \cdot 16bit = 881159112894555448960 bits \approx 8.812e20 bits $$
    
    Fliprate required to run the model:
    
    $$ 55 072 444 555 909 715 560 \cdot 1000Hz \cdot 2748 bits = 151 339 077 639 639 898 358 880 000 bits/s \approx 1.5134e26 bits/s $$

    \section{Misc. properties}
    
    Working memory is simply the sum of the working memories of all brain nodes:
    
    $$ 15 \cdot 4 + 7 = 67 $$
    
    Learning rate gives the statistical probability of her succesfully retaining the ability to solve a test task on the learning rate portion of the idala screenings.
    
    UEIA has learning rate of 0.05, meaning that correspondingly the probability of NOT learning how to complete the test task is 0.95.
    Felicity's learning rate is set to 0.2 so the corresponding probability of failure is 0.8.
    
    Each mind independently rolls for the chance to remember how to complete the task so for serendipity as a whole to fail, every single mind independently has to fail as well. The probability of that is just the product of the individual minds' failure chances:
    
    $$ 0.95^{15} \cdot 0.8 = 0.370633 $$
    
    And thus the chance of success is the oposite of that:
    
    $$ 1 - 0.370633 = 0.629367 \approx 0.63 $$

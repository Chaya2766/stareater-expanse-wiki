	\medskip
	
	[?] Felicity is a human mind upload with different structure from typical AI models, as such one synapse is not the same as one parameter.
	This is the only such model in this composition.
	The number of synapses comes from the average values for the human brain of 86 billion neurons total and 7000 synapses per neuron.
	The number of parameters comes from the particular digital emulation of the organic neurons which is much larger than just one parameter per synapse.
	
	\bigskip
	
	Human-Ueia bridge synapse count is calculated in accordance with the 1/215 rule which says that the bridge network usually has around 215 times fewer synapses than the model it is to be used with.
	Since this one bridges two different their size is averaged first.
	
	$$ \frac{1125899906842624 + 602000000000000}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{215} = 4018371876378 $$
    
    \bigskip
    
    All of these models are meant to be inferenced at 1000Hz.
    
    All of them use 16-bit parameters.
    
    $$ 4018371876378 \cdot 16 bits \approx 6.4294e13 bits $$
    
    Given the 2748 bit flips per 16-bit multiply-accumulate operation:
    
    $$ 4018371876378 \cdot 1000Hz \cdot 2748bits \approx 1.104e19 bits/s $$

	\section{Composite mind structure}
	
    The composite mind is fully-connected meaning that every brain node has a direct connection to every other brain node via a bridge, and every bridge has a direct connection to every other bridge via a meta-bridge.
    
    \medskip
    
    The number of brain nodes is 64, as such the number of bridges is:
    
    $$ N_B = \frac{64 \cdot (64-1)}{2} = 2016 $$
    
    And the number of meta-bridges in that case is:
    
    $$ N_{MB} = \frac{2016 \cdot (2016-1)}{2} = 2\ 031\ 120 $$
    
    \bigskip
    
    There is only one human brain in the network, and the other 63 brain nodes are UEIA models, as such all bridges from Human to UEIA will be the UEIA-Human bridges.
    There are 63 UEIA-Human bridges.
    
    \medskip
    
    All meta-bridges that connect the UEIA-Human bridges are going to also be UEIA-Human bridges, so the number of UEIA-Human metabridges is:
    
    $$ \frac{63\cdot(63-1)}{2} = 1953 $$
    
    The remaining ones can be UEIA bridges, so their number (bridges and meta-bridges) is:
    
    $$ N_{UB} = 2016 - 63 = 1953 $$
    
    $$ N+{UMB} = 2031120 - 1953 = 2\ 029\ 167 $$

    \section{computational requirements}
    
    The whole network is to be inferenced at 1000Hz.
    
    All parameters are 16-bit.
    
    A 16-bit multiply-accumulate operation requires 2748 bit flips.
    
    \bigskip
    
    Size of the model:
    
    $$ 37 902 565 761 467 419 840 \cdot 16bit = 606441052183478717440 bits \approx 6.065e20 bits $$
    
    Fliprate required to run the model:
    
    $$ 37 902 565 761 467 419 840 \cdot 1000Hz \cdot 2748 bits = 104156250712512469720320000 bits/s \approx 1.0416e26 bits/s $$
